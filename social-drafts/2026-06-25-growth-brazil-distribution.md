## Why this piece is a return-rate asset (the growth read)

A slow publication's growth problem is the **second visit**. This piece is built for it on two fronts.

1. **A frame the reader re-uses.** "A country's literacy number rose to 66% — graded by the same school systems it measures; for the same year the independent exam read 49.3% against the indicator's 56%; a serious state published both" is a lens a reader carries to *every* official statistic they meet next. That re-use — not volume — is what brings someone back to the archive and makes them forward it.
2. **It opens Latin America.** Brazil is only the corpus's **second LatAm piece** (after Uruguay) and the **first in Edition 02**. That widens the map onto a query cluster the MENA-heavy corpus can't reach — the discoverability argument below.

The strongest distribution line is the spine, stated plainly:

> Brazil made one state's literacy model national and pinned a single number to it. It rose three years running, to 66%. But that number is scored by the same school systems it grades — and for 2023, where it read 56%, the government's *own* external exam read 49.3%. The honest part isn't the 66%. It's that Brazil printed the 49.3% next to it.

## CORRECTION — 2026-09-23. Both captions carried a figure this piece does not ship, and the wrong pair beside it.

Found by **standing assertion 23 (`qa_packet_figures`)** on the day it was built, running over
every distribution artefact in `social-drafts/**` rather than only over the held wave packet. This
was one of three defects it returned, and the other two were the instrument's own scoping, not the
copy's. **This one is real, it is about a live piece, and it was staged ready to post.**

**What the captions said.** EN primary: *"The federal government's own external exam reads **49%**."*
EN short: *"Brazil prints both (**66% vs 49%**)."* AR primary: «أمّا الامتحان الاتحادي الخارجي
فيقرأ **49٪**.»

**What the piece ships.** *"for 2023 the state-run Indicador read **56%** while the externally-run
Saeb read **49.3%**, a gap of seven points the education ministry has acknowledged in public."*
Both languages carry **49.3**.

**Two errors, and the second is the worse one.**
1. **49.3 was rounded to 49** in the advertising copy for a piece whose argument is that two
   instruments must be read at their own precision. A figure rounded in a caption is a figure
   composed by the caption writer.
2. **The short caption paired 66% with 49%** — the 2025 Indicador against the 2023 Saeb — while the
   piece pairs **2023 with 2023** on purpose, because that is the only pairing that isolates the
   instrument from the year. Rulings **#21** (vintage) and **#24** (position) committed in the
   distribution copy of the piece that exists to explain them, and the gap it advertises (17
   points) is more than twice the gap the piece reports (seven).

**Fixed in both languages**, from the piece's own sentence: the EN and AR primaries now read *"For
2023, where the indicator read 56%, the federal government's own external exam read 49.3%"*, and
the short caption reads *"Brazil prints both — 56% and 49.3% for the same year."*

**What the repository can and cannot say about this.** It can say the captions were staged as ready
on 2026-06-25 and sat uncorrected for **90 days**. It cannot say whether either was ever posted —
Growth does not post, and nothing in this repository records what went out. That asymmetry is the
reason the check now gates the deploy rather than living in a posture note.

**Three more instances of the same figure sat OUTSIDE the caption fences and the check could not
see them** — the growth read's own frame line, and the "strongest distribution line, stated
plainly" that the packet offers for verbatim use. All three are corrected above by hand, found by
reading the file the assertion had just marked clean. **That is this assertion's scope limit,
stated on day one rather than discovered in three months:** the fence defines the perimeter, so a
figure one line outside a fence is outside the check, and this file keeps postable copy in exactly
that position. Named as the forward question in today's QA log.

**Owed:** the corrected **Arabic** caption is composed, not gated. It goes to the Arabic Editor with
the wave packet's five, which makes **six captions** owed a gate.

**And the note this correction supersedes.** The 2026-06-12 Husseiny tile file states, as a binding
posture note, *"captions never carry figures the article doesn't."* It has been true of that tile
and it was not true here, written in the same voice, three weeks apart. **A contract in prose is a
declaration (#37)** — and it took an assertion, not a note, to find the exception.
