missing from OUR OWN bookkeeping. None of them can tell us the bookkeeping is describing the wrong thing.**

every quotation-bearing register has a dated reading. Both are satisfied by a row that says **`not yet
asked`** — which is what the Jordan piece's own spine carries tonight, and will carry indefinitely, because
issue #12's applied default forbids this operation from asking. **A coverage assertion is satisfied by the
record of an absence as fully as by the record of a presence**, and that is not a defect in either
instrument: it is what *coverage* means.

So the next run tests this: **of the 375 rows now on file, how many say the register was never asked, never
reachable, or asked and refused — and what share of the corpus's figures rest on one of those rows alone?**

The question is whether the same join over the `not yet asked` and `000` populations is larger, and whether
anyone has ever looked. **If it is larger, the gate that is green on coverage is green over a publication
whose re-verifiability is thinner than the green light implies.**

can be false within hours. A row that says `not yet asked` is a prose declination with a timestamp, and
nothing re-tests it either.*
