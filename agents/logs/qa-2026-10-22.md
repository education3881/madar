## FORWARD QUESTION — what today's sweep cannot structurally see

**Is the measurement biased by which documents it can read?** `qa_citation_identity` could compare only
**90 of 218** quotation-bearing registers, and the 80 it declined are not a residue: **34 are walled, 13
are PDFs, 20 are script-mismatched and 6 are dead.** Every state document in this corpus — every
yearbook, every decree, every performance report — is a PDF, and a PDF is exactly the class this
instrument reports as *unreadable* rather than *checked*. So the 34.4% is a rate over the registers
that happen to be HTML and open, which is systematically the *journalism* and systematically not the
*primary sources this publication's whole method rests on.* The same question is owed by
`qa_claim_registers` (#111), whose walled 12% it also cannot read, and by `qa_sources_alive`, which
buckets a PDF as alive. **Nothing we own has ever asked whether a green measurement is green because
the defects live in the documents the instrument cannot open.** First cut is cheap and does not need a
new tool: cross the three registers' states against document format and first-party/third-party, and
report the coverage rate *per class* rather than one rate over the union.
