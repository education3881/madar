## 6. Forward question — what can today's sweep not structurally see?

`qa_live_drift` closes the built-vs-served gap for **pages**. It does not close
it for the **deploy pipeline's health between runs**: drift tells us the origin
is behind, not *why* — a waiting push and a dead deploy produce the same
signal until someone reads the Actions API. The July outage was distinguishable
only there. Next increment: when drift is nonzero AND `origin/main` equals
local HEAD (i.e. the push happened), the check should consult the Actions API
(reachable via fetch, per the 07-06 finding) and name the failing step —
build-gate or deploy-gate (#16/#17) — instead of leaving the diagnosis to a
human reading a dashboard. Separately: the feed verification ran from *this*
sandbox's network position; a reader behind a corporate proxy or an RSS service
(Feedly fetches from its own crawlers) may see different behaviour — we have
verified one consumer, not the class.
