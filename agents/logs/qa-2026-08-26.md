## 1. Defects found this run

### P1 — the five non-article pages promised a share card and had none (93 days live)

`Base.astro` emitted `twitter:card = summary_large_image` **unconditionally**,
next to an `og:image` emitted **conditionally**. Every page therefore promised a
large card; only the 77 pages that pass `ogImage` had one.

Missing on: `/`, `/ar/`, `/editions/`, `/ar/editions/`, `/about/` — **including
both front doors**, which are the URLs a reader is most likely to share.

This is the **2026-08-18 defect in a second costume**. That fix generated 38
raster cards and asserted no `og:image` may be an SVG; it could not catch this,
because it enumerated *articles*, and every article has an image. Sixth
statement of *a green check is scoped to what it enumerates*.

**Fixed two ways, and the second is the fix of record:**
1. `/og/brand-card.png` (28 KB, 1200×600) wired into all five pages.
2. `twitter:card` is now **derived** — `{ogImageAbs ? 'summary_large_image' : 'summary'}`.
   A page without a card can no longer promise one. Filed as **ruling #36**.

`qa_consumer_surface` gains two assertions: every served page has an `og:image`;
`twitter:card` and `og:image` agree **in both directions** (an image shipped
under a `summary` card is also a defect — it wastes an asset we drew).

**Proof, per #35 — both halves in one run:** against the pre-fix `dist` the new
assertion reports **10 defects across exactly the 5 known-broken pages** and is
**silent on all 77 pages already known to be correct**.

### P2 — `qa_reachability` walked one language and reported on two

Yesterday's assertion started at `/index.html` — the **English** front door — so
it proved that every page is reachable *by a reader willing to pass through
English pages*. An Arabic reader arrives at `/ar/`. An Arabic article linked
only from an English hub would have passed.

Now walks **per language root, confined to that language's pages**, with the
whole-site walk kept as a third pass for language-neutral pages (VALENCE).

**Proof, per #35 — control first, then bite:**
- **Control (real build): PASS.** The Arabic subgraph is complete on first
  contact — **40/40, max click-depth 2**, better than the English side's 3.
- **Bite (article links severed from the two Arabic hubs): 12 AR-side orphans,
  while the whole-site pass still reports 0.** That gap *is* the blind spot.

### P3 — the 08-25 QA log carried no forward question

The 2026-08-23 ruling makes the blind-spot question a **required written output**
of every QA pass, not a good habit — three findings in three days came from it.
The 08-25 log ends at the gate. Named here rather than quietly corrected.

### Carried, not closed — the feeds

## 5. Forward question — what can today's sweep not structurally see?

**Every check we own reads `dist`, which is the output of *one* build, on *one*
machine, at *one* moment. Nothing has ever compared what we built to what is
actually being served.** The 08-25 `lastmod` defect was caught because it was
reasoned about; had it shipped, every local check would have stayed green while
the deploy failed, exactly as the 07-02→07-06 outage did for four days. And
today's state verification found the live site three days stale — which no
assertion reported, because no assertion looks at the live site at all.

**Tomorrow, before adding anything new:** fetch the deployed pages and diff them
against the local `dist` — page count, sitemap `lastmod`, and the head of a
sampled article in each language. A build is a claim about what readers receive;
**#36 says a claim must be derived from the thing it describes, and the thing
here is the live origin, not our filesystem.**
