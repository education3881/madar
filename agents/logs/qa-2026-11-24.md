## 2. ITEM 50 CLOSED — four instruments ask whether a quoted string is IN a document; now one asks which form it is

The row, raised 2026-10-28, was measured rather than hypothesised: `restofworld.org` serves *both* `South
Korea's AI textbooks fail after rushed rollout` (its `<title>`) and `AI-powered textbooks fail to make the
grade in South Korea` (its `<h1>`), and **both are in the document**, so a substring search over served
text confirms a `<title>`-derived quotation forever and cannot turn red on the wrong form. #119 says a
quotation mark promises a **named form**; every instrument we own flattened the document to text before
comparing.

**The fix needed no network, which is why the row called it the first cut.** The forms are in the bytes
`qa_citation_identity` already fetches, and only `served_text()` was throwing them away by stripping the
tags before anybody looked. Shipped today, in `compare()`:

- `headline_forms()` reads five of the document's own statements of its own name out of the **raw bytes** —
  `<h1>`, `<title>`, `og:title`, JSON-LD `headline`, and the de-slugified URL path. Each form holds a
  **list**, because a document with two `<h1>`s has two things it leads with and picking one would be the
  instrument inventing the answer.
- `classify_form()` returns the **strongest** standing a token can claim, best first: `h1` **evidence** ·
  `body` **running text** (in the served text and in no headline form — the ordinary, healthy case) ·
  `title` / `og:title` / `headline` **warning** · `slug` **COUNTERFEIT**.
- A token **absent from the served text** is now checked against the forms too, so a token that lives only
  in the address is reported as the counterfeit it is rather than as an absence.

**PRINTED AND NOT ASSERTED**, for the reason the `absent` bucket is printed: the form a register leads with
belongs to its publisher, a `title`-only match is a warning about **our** annotation rather than a defect in
**their** document, and the repair runs through a human re-read (#93).

**WHAT IS ASSERTED, and it is in our lane:** a `found` token in a row observed on or after
`FORM_EPOCH = 2026-11-24` must **carry its form**, so an emit that silently stops recording forms fails the
build instead of the census quietly printing a smaller total. **103 rows predate the field and are DECLARED
rather than reconciled backward** — the count prints on every check, because a point-of-filing rule fixes a
count's future and leaves a step discontinuity where it was written (2026-10-04, limb 2).

**The register converges by itself:** a row whose confirmed token has no form is now `owed`, so the emit's
own budget tops it up across runs (#111's pattern) and a row re-read for its form is re-read for its

**And a fourth apparent cause was MY OWN EXTRACTOR.** `og:title` read as *The teacher in Bo who still uses

captured and required to close), and proved on the instance that found it: that page's `og:title` reads
whole and the token classifies `h1` with all four forms listed. **A limb aimed at somebody else's documents
was debugged by aiming it at ours, which is the cheapest control this operation owns and it is sitting in
