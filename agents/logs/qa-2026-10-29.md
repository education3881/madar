## Forward question — what today's sweep cannot structurally see

**Every instrument this operation owns asks whether what we QUOTED is still there. None asks whether the
register has said something NEW since we read it.**

`qa_sources_alive` asks whether a URL answers. `qa_claim_registers` asks whether anybody can ever re-open
it. `qa_citation_identity` asks whether our quoted string is still in the bytes. **All three are satisfied,
green, by a document that has since published a correction, a result or a retraction on the exact question

claim.** A cookie banner moves every fetch. So the output is a **reading list ordered by change**, not an
