## 5. NEW this run — the raster-format assertion (og cards)

**What the existing sweeps could not see.** Yesterday's forward question was: *the sweeps check that links resolve and that pages are reachable; neither checks that a resolving link goes somewhere useful.* This is the first instance found by asking it.

Every article's `og:image` pointed at its hero still — **an SVG**. The file exists, it is the right image, the URL is absolute, and it resolved on every check we have ever run. **No major social consumer renders SVG cards**: Facebook, X, LinkedIn and Slack accept PNG, JPEG, WEBP and GIF only. So for 83 days every share of a Madār article — in both languages — has travelled with **no card image at all**, while the QA log reported the og:image as present and resolving. The check was green and the reader saw nothing. Same family as the flag-sweep inversion (08-09), the chrome 404 (08-16) and the orphan page (08-17): *a green check is scoped to what it enumerates.*

Aggravating detail: the pages also declare `twitter:card = summary_large_image`, which promises a large image and delivered none.

**Fix shipped this run.**
- `public/og/*.png` — **38 cards**, one per still, rendered at **1200×600** from the same SVGs (sharp / librsvg 2.61, density 144, paper `#FAFAF7` flatten, palette PNG). **Total 452 KB, average 10 KB** — line art compresses to almost nothing.
- Native **1200×600 (2:1)**, not padded to 1200×630: X crops `summary_large_image` to 2:1 anyway, and padding put visible paper bars on the stills that carry a sand ground. **No crop** — the guidebook's pad-never-crop rule is honoured by not needing either.
- Both article routes now derive the card path (`/stills/x.svg` → `/og/x.png`) with the reason written into the source comment, so the next person to touch `ogImage` sees why.
- Arabic shaping verified **by looking at the PNG**, per the standing rule: librsvg ships harfbuzz + fribidi, and سكون renders joined.
- **New standing assertion:** *no `og:image` may end in `.svg`.* Runs every QA pass. Result this run: **39 og:image URLs, 0 SVG, 0 unresolved.**

**Also shipped: VALENCE's card** (the item yesterday flagged as the highest-leverage one left). A bespoke line-art still in the Madār palette — a lattice of elements, one bond in kiln orange — at `public/stills/valence-elements-of-good-education.svg`, rendered to `public/og/`, wired into the VALENCE head with `og:image`, dimensions, alt text and `twitter:image`. **11 KB.** One rendering defect caught and fixed by inspecting the output: `letter-spacing` on the Arabic wordmark broke the joins (مدار rendering as م د ا ر) — tracking is never applied to Arabic; re-rendered and re-inspected at 3× zoom.
