## 1. The forward question, tested before anything new was added

Carried from yesterday, verbatim: **does every figure in `social-drafts/**` and in
`content-drafts/**` appear in the shipped text of the piece it names?**

**It bit.** The answer to the first half is no, and it is no about a **live** piece, not a
held one.

**Half the question is answered NO on purpose.** `content-drafts/**` is deliberately out of
scope and the reason is in the tool's header: a recon, a verdict and a commission are
*working* documents whose job is to record figures the piece does **not** carry — the
rejected value, the fenced pair, the reproduction that disagrees. Requiring their figures to
appear in the piece would invert their function. **A distribution caption is the one artefact
in this repository that speaks as the publication**, and it is the one that takes the rule.

### Standing assertion 23 — `qa_packet_figures`

Every numeral inside a `<!-- caption:en -->` / `<!-- caption:ar -->` fence must appear in the
**shipped text** — title, dek, date, edition, body — of the piece named by the entry's
`<!-- piece: slug -->` marker, **in that caption's own language**.

**`sources[]` is excluded, and that exclusion is the check.** The annotations are shipped, but
they carry figures the piece does not claim, so including them would let a struck figure keep
passing because its own correction note still names it — the 09-14 masking trap, *where does
the string I am looking for also legitimately appear?*

**Binding is declared, never inferred.** The slug is written into the file. A binding a
machine has to guess from a heading is a binding that unbinds silently the next time somebody
edits a title (#37).
