## 1. Defects found this run

### P1 — Latin-script controlled vocabulary on every Arabic page (live 90 days)
**Found:** the daily sweep, extended past well-formedness into "what does the consumer receive."
**Scope:** all 38 Arabic article pages + AR home + AR editions index + the Arabic RSS feed.
**Symptom:** byline meta, marginalia «المرجع الميداني», related-reading meta, listing country tags and feed `<category>` all rendered `Sierra Leone · Africa`, `K-12`, `value of teachers` to an Arabic reader.
**Why every existing check passed:** parity counted 38 files against 38 files; build clean; links resolved; 476 hreflang refs reciprocal; `lang="ar"` and `dir="rtl"` present **and correct**. The offending line literally declares the content Arabic and then interpolates English.
**Fix:** `web/src/lib/i18n-geo.ts` display layer (schema untouched, no article edited, EN identical, fail-loud on unmapped keys). Wired into the AR article route, AR index, AR editions index, `RelatedReading` (which serves both languages from one component and did not know which it was in), and the AR feed.
**Filed as ruling #33.**

### P1 — Arabic feed carried no direction metadata (live since the feed shipped, 08-16)
**Symptom:** 27 of 38 AR deks mix Arabic with Latin or digits; zero direction metadata anywhere in either feed. Under an LTR base paragraph — the default in most readers — trailing punctuation and digit runs resolve to the wrong side of the string.
**Fix:** U+200F RLM prefix on AR titles and categories (`<title>` is plain text by spec, so a control character is the only lever); `<div dir="rtl" lang="ar">` on AR descriptions (markup where markup is available).

### P2 — feeds carried no art
**Fix:** `<enclosure>` on all 76 items across both feeds, pointing at the 08-18 raster cards. Raster only — the 08-18 no-SVG rule binds the feed as it binds `og:image`. Assertion checks extension **and** on-disk existence.

### P2 — zero structured data across all 82 pages
**Fix (Growth):** `web/src/lib/structuredData.ts`; `schema.org/Article` on all 76 article pages. See §3.

### P3 — cross-language link missing `lang`
The Arabic page's `English version ↗` carried `hreflang="en"` but no `lang`/`dir`, while the English page's «النسخة العربية ↗» had carried `lang="ar" dir="rtl"` since launch. A screen reader on an Arabic page never switched voice. Fixed.

### Checked and found CLEAN (recorded so it is not re-investigated)

- `robots.txt` — present, `Allow: /`, correct absolute sitemap reference.

- 8 Latin-script hits inside Arabic article bodies — **all legitimate** under #28a (institution names and source-citation URLs: *Universidad de Santiago de Chile*, *India TV News (PTI)*, *Uruguay XXI*). Explicitly excluded from the new assertion.
