## 3 · A false green, found by accident, and it extends open item 8

`qa_live_drift`, run **before** the content commit, reported **`CLEAN — 134 URLs, 0 lastmod drift, 4
sampled heads identical`** — while eight article files on disk had changed.

The cause is **open item 8's cause exactly**: the sitemap's `<lastmod>` resolver reads **git history**,
the build reads the **working tree**, and an uncommitted file is invisible to the resolver. So the
local build emitted the same `<lastmod>` the origin was already serving, the two matched, and the
instrument said nothing had changed.

**But the symptom is the mirror of the one item 8 describes.** Item 8 is written about `--check`
reporting *silent content change* on **every** page after a chrome edit — a flood of false positives.
Today's observation is a **false negative** on a content edit, in a **second instrument**, from the
same root cause. Rebuilt after the commit and re-read: the eight repaired pages carry
`2026-10-14T09:51:17Z`, and `qa_live_drift` reports **`DRIFT(53)`** against the origin — the eight
pages plus the 45 index, browse and chrome pages whose dates the resolver propagates. **That is the
correct reading and it is what a reader is waiting on until today's deploy fires.**

Item 8's scope widens accordingly, recorded on the queue: *the defect is not a property of
`qa_served_manifest --check`; it is a property of comparing a git-derived date against a working-tree
build, and it produces false positives on chrome edits and false negatives on content edits.*
