## The head — item 144, measured, repaired, proved both ways, and gated

**BEFORE.** `overflow_population.py . --widths 390` → **17 of 162 artefacts overflowing**: 14 weekly
reviews (+466, +438, +391, +329, +319, +288, +243, +220, +215, +163, +163, +101, +41, +34 px) and 3 briefs
(+90, +36, +12 px). `qa_artefact_render` green on all 162 of the same files at 1280 at the same moment.

**THE CAUSE, in two mechanisms rather than one.** Diagnosed from the files, not guessed:

1. **Reviews — `td.n { white-space: nowrap }` and `<pre>` with `white-space: pre`.** A cell or a preformatted
   block that may not wrap sets a min-content width wider than its column, and **an element whose
   min-content width exceeds its column grows its own box instead of overflowing it**, which is why the
   leaf probe returned the `<section>` with no overflowing descendant. The longest single token in eight of
   the fourteen is the founder's own push path, `"/Users/vini/Documents/Claude/Projects/Educational
   Website"`, 50 characters inside a `pre`.
2. **Briefs — one unbreakable token in prose.** `/content-drafts/verdicts/ar/2026-05-28-kuwait-…` (69 ch),
   `https://education3881.github.io/madar/` (38), `border-color:var(--color-accent)` (32).
3. **A third, found only by repairing the first two:** `.statgrid { grid-template-columns: repeat(4, 1fr) }`
   — `1fr` is `minmax(auto, 1fr)`, so the min-content floor keeps four columns on a 320px box. It appeared
   in the measurement only after the tables stopped dominating the worst-case figure.

**THE REPAIR — one `@media (max-width: 640px)` block, applied to all 162 artefacts and not to the 17.**
Three reasons, each of them a rule this operation already owns: there **is no template** (item 79 — 162
independent copies of one stylesheet, so the copy a future run inherits must be the fixed one, and today's
weekly review runs after this commit and copies `2026-11-15-weekly-review.html`); the two recent clean
reviews are **clean by luck** and not by construction (11-15 carries 8 tables against 09-06's 1); and *a
count that stops at the newest instance cannot tell a repaired population from a lucky one*, which is the
row's own sentence.

**It took three passes and the first two are worth recording, because both were specificity, not design.**
v1 (`th, td { overflow-wrap: anywhere }`) took 17 → 12; the `td.n` nowrap rule is **more specific than
`td`**, and a media query adds no specificity. v2 added `white-space: normal` to `th, td` and moved
nothing — same reason. v3 names `td.n, th.n` in the selector list and trims cell padding to 0.35rem at
narrow widths: **17 → 0.**
