## FORWARD QUESTION — what can today's sweep not structurally see?

**Which of our gating assertions read a SAMPLE rather than a population, and how many instances live
outside each sample?**

This is not a hypothesis. `qa_font_payload` shipped this morning with a header stating that its six-page
set is a sample and that a defect living only outside it is outside the gate. **By the afternoon the
corpus census had found twelve live instances of the exact defect the gate was built for, on pages the
gate does not load.** The caveat became a measurement inside twelve hours.

It generalises at once, and nobody has asked it: `qa_render` shoots **4 targets of 134**;
`qa_arabic_shaping` and `qa_arabic_joining` read a chosen set; `qa_feed_direction` reads one item;
`qa_artefact_render` renders **all 127** artefacts. One of those is a population, four are samples, and
**not one states its coverage as a fraction.** First cut needs no new tool: print `N of M` beside each
verdict, the way `qa_bridge_coverage` prints 2.3% rather than implying 100%. Second cut: run each sampled
check over its full population **once**, by hand, and record how many instances were outside.

*Six rulings say a green check is scoped to what it enumerates. All six ask what the check looked at.
None asks **how much of it**, and a green sample reads exactly like a green population.*
