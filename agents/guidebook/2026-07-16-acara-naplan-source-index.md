## Rules of the instrument (carry in the sentence, per #19)

1. **The time series restarts at 2023.** ACARA's own rule: no comparison across the reset — four proficiency levels (Exceeding / Strong / Developing / Needs additional support) replaced the ten bands and the 2008 national minimum standard on 10 Feb 2023. Any pre/post pairing must name the break.
2. **"Below Strong" is two populations.** The harsh rendering ("1 in 3 below the bar") adds Developing (defined: *working towards* expectations) to Needs additional support. State the derivation; never render Developing as "failing" — that misreading is the live 2025–26 controversy (AERO decade-of-writing analysis → media "failing" claims → AARE counter).
3. **Third-party analyses of NAPLAN data are not ACARA statistics.** AERO/AARE/media figures attribute as analysis, with the primary beside them.
4. **Release cadence:** testing March (moved from May at the reset) → preliminary to schools ~April → final summaries mid-June → student reports Term 3 → **national results late July/August**.
