**Filed:** 2026-07-28 · **For:** any future England/UK-schools accountability, inspection, or assessment-label piece. Sibling of the ACARA/NAPLAN index (2026-07-16, row 11) and the Oman school-evaluation index (2026-06-16). **Remit note up front:** Ofsted inspects **England only** — Scotland (Education Scotland/HMIE), Wales (Estyn) and Northern Ireland (ETI) keep their own inspectorates. ## Registers, by load

1. **The instrument of record — GOV.UK / Ofsted.**
   - Renewed Education Inspection Framework, in force **10 Nov 2025**: `gov.uk/government/publications/education-inspection-framework/education-inspection-framework-for-use-from-november-2025`. The toolkits (state-funded schools, EY, independent, FE, ITE) carry the operative rules — grade bands, the "secure fit" rule, evaluation areas.
   - Design confirmation, 9 Sep 2025: `gov.uk/government/news/ofsted-confirms-changes-to-education-inspection-and-unveils-new-look-report-cards`. Five bands: *urgent improvement / needs attention / expected standard / strong standard / exceptional*; safeguarding met/not-met; inclusion a standalone area.
   - **Ofsted education-inspection blog** (`educationinspection.blog.gov.uk`) — **fetches clean as served text through our path** (verified 07-28: the 12 Jun 2026 Owston post read in full, verbatim-quotable). This is the primary voice channel for policy-intent quotes from named Ofsted directors. Prefer it over trade-press paraphrase for load-bearing operator quotes.
   - Published report cards live at `reports.ofsted.gov.uk` (per-school; useful to spot-check a specific card, not for aggregate tallies).

2. **The aggregate + survey layer — trade press (Schools Week / Tes / FE Week).**
   - **Schools Week (`schoolsweek.co.uk`) fetches clean as served text through our path** (verified 07-28: the 22 Jun 2026 survey piece read in full — every figure verbatim). This is the fastest route to (a) grade-distribution tallies from published cards, (b) independent-survey figures, (c) named-operator quotes. Treat it as **citation-of-record for figures that only exist in analysis of the cards** (Ofsted publishes cards, not a distribution table) — but state "Schools Week analysis of published Ofsted report cards" in the sentence (#19), because the tally is the outlet's, the cards are Ofsted's.
   - Tes (`tes.com`) carries FFT Education Datalab analyses (e.g. the 1,360-card read) — corroborates Schools Week; useful as the second register for a distribution figure.
   - **Aggregate counts** (e.g. "1,360 state schools inspected by end of May 2026") are **Ofsted figures reported via trade press** — carry the vintage ("by end of May 2026") and the tier ("an Ofsted figure, via Schools Week"). Do not upgrade to a primary Ofsted publication unless one is found.

3. **The courts — judiciary.uk.**
   - `judiciary.uk` carries the NAHT v Ofsted judgment PDF (5 Nov 2025, Saini J). Quote the judgment for the "not matters for this court" / "policy choice and system design" language; trade press restates it. The **appeal** (lost, Nov 2025) is a separate step from the original refusal — name which one failed.

## Figure-integrity traps specific to this beat
- **Grade counts are two populations.** The first-wave 150 grades came from **22 volunteer schools** — a self-selecting cohort that skews positive by construction (ASCL and Ofsted both say so). Any early distribution must carry "volunteer" in the same sentence, or it manufactures a rosy read. The scale figure (1,360 by end-May) is the honest denominator once inspections became routine (resumed 1 Dec 2025).
- **Survey rates are conditioned on the sample.** The Coffield/Tymms/Pascal/Norris/Richards survey is **100 self-selected heads**, response-rate-skewed (trusts 7.9% vs council 24.5%). Every rate (70% negative wellbeing, 64% not an improvement, 8 quitting, 53% grades fair, 14 positive) is "of 100," never of 1,360 — ruling **#25** (a conditional rate carries its condition). Ofsted's rival instrument is the **IFF Research** evaluation (universal, commissioned, due **2027**) plus Ofsted's own post-inspection survey (no narrative responses; positive-experience schools over-represented). The Ed04 move: name both instruments and why their designs predict different readings.
- **"Not comparable to old grades."** Ofsted insists the new bands are not the old four-point scale renamed — avoid "exceptional = outstanding" mappings.
- **Frame-sibling with Oman.** The Oman piece (`2026-06-20-oman-school-performance-ratings`) owns the single-word-retreat story (Ruth Perry → 2024 scrapping). An England piece gets **one orienting Ruth Perry sentence** and then lives in the replacement's operating period (Nov 2025→). Cross-link, don't re-narrate.
