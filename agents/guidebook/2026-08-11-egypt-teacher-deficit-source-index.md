## 2. Method note — **a deficit is a ratio, not a headcount** (third shape of the scoped-instrument family)
Row 15: a pledge is a scoped instrument. Row 19: a multiplier without a base is a scope missing its scope. Today's shape: **a deficit-closure claim is unreadable without the deficit's formula.** A teacher deficit = hours required ÷ hours per teacher — so it closes three ways: add people (appointments), add *lesson-sellers* (per-session corps — headcount without posts), or **shrink the denominator** (longer year, more weeks, lower weekly quota). Egypt's Dec-2025 "eliminated" claim blends all three. Binding discipline for the draft: (a) every closure claim carries **which instrument closed what share**; (b) a deficit figure carries its **vintage** — the headline drifts 469k (plan) ↔ 460k (closure) between registers (#21/#23); (c) instruments that move the *denominator* make the series non-comparable across years — never present pre- and post-quota deficits as one series (#24); (d) the *absence* of a published formula is itself reportable.

## 3. Funnel discipline (the #14 axis, Egypt instance)
Five distinct numbers, never chained: competition **target** (30,000/yr) ≠ **sat** (90,000) ≠ **passed** (28,000 phase 1; 20,866 phase 2) ≠ **cleared the second screening** (Military Academy — thousands excluded; EIPR documents weight/pregnancy exclusions, appeals live) ≠ **appointed** (not yet located in any primary — the missing number of the whole story). The 2025 competition's waiting lists were still being drawn in Jul 2026: the appointment pipeline runs years behind the closure claim. That lag is checkable and usable.
