## Method note — a multiplier without a base is a scoped instrument missing its scope (extends row 15)
Row 15 (England) filed: *a pledge is a scoped instrument — progress figure and scope-definition-with-date are one citable pair.* The UAE corpus adds the sibling case: **a ninefold increase with no disclosed baseline**. A multiplier is a ratio whose denominator is someone else's to reveal; until the base is on the record, the honest sentence is "a ninefold increase on an undisclosed base," and the undisclosed base is itself reportable. The general family now has three shapes on file: progress-against-scoped-target (England 4,654/6,500), **multiplier-without-base** (UAE ninefold), and absolute-with-distant-deadline-and-unknown-baseline (Dubai 3,000 by 2033). All ride #21/#22/#25.

## Bilingual-register asset (the AR composition inverts)
Every load-bearing primary has an **official Arabic edition** (WAM AR, Media Office AR, u.ae AR). Discipline inverts accordingly: the AR composer's trap here is not transliteration but **version drift** — the AR and EN editions of the same release are compositions, not translations of each other, and figures must be traced in whichever edition is cited. Trace EN quotes to the EN edition, AR quotes to the AR edition; never cross-attribute.
