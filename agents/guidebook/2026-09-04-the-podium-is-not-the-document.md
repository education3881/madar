# The podium is not the document (row 38 · ruling #43)

**Filed:** 2026-09-04, on the re-verification of Ed05 recon 6 (the best system in Africa, founder-mandated slot #3) before commission.
**Owner:** Researcher (the rule), Editor (application at commission), Verifier (the trace row).
**Extends:** #38 (a source can drift against itself — this is the same drift *between* an owner's two registers rather than inside one document), #40 (a mirror can misname the instrument), #41 (a URL is a figure cited as fetched; a search summary is not a served register), #21 (vintage), §1 row 23 / #29 (a ranking is not a measurement). Joins the **internal-consistency family** and touches the pledge family (rows 15/19/20).

## The rule

**When an owner's launch remarks and the owner's own published report disagree about what a number means, the report rides and the podium is reportable only as the disagreement.** A press conference, a ministerial keynote, a "highlights" quote at the launch — these are the owner's *voice*, and a voice is a register with its own vintage, audience and incentive. The document is the owner's *finding*. The two are the same source in name and not in evidentiary weight; a figure that carries "the department said" as its warrant when the department's report says otherwise has been laundered through its own owner.

## Origin case — one report, two readings, eighteen days apart

The 08-16 recon carried South Africa's TIMSS 2023 Grade 9 mathematics result as *"+8 points, 389 → 397, 2019–2023"*, framed by the coverage as a *"remarkable upward trend"* — a phrase from the launch (News24 quoting the Department of Basic Education's director of national assessment). Recorded as search-stage, homework assigned: read the national report.

Read today, the DBE's own **TIMSS 2023 Highlights Report** says, of the same two numbers: *"these improvements for both mathematics and science were not statistically significant, indicating that achievement levels have remained relatively stable between these two cycles"* — and carries, in its own footnote, the IEA's reliability reservation on the Grade 8 mathematics result (*"percentage of learners with achievement too low for estimation exceeds 15% but does not exceed 25%"*). The document's word for +8 is **stable**; its frame is resilience against an *expected* post-COVID decline, which is a real and interesting accomplishment and a different one from improvement. The same report gives the participating count at the fourth grade as **59** countries; the coverage's "58" was a mirror's count (#40).

Nothing here was false at the podium. The director's line — "a positive story at the Grade 9 level" — is defensible *as a spoken gloss on stability where decline was expected*. It is not defensible as the figure's warrant, because the figure's warrant is the report, and the report declines to call it improvement.

## Three corollaries, each bitten in the same re-verification

1. **A count reported by press is a mirror's count.** 58 → 59. The primary's count rides; when the piece cannot read the primary, it does not carry the count at all ("last of the participating countries" is true under both).
2. **A schedule is a plan, and a plan is a pledge** (rows 15/19/20 family). The owner's own slides (PASEC coordinator, UIS GAML working group) planned international reports *in 2025* and national reports *in Q1 2026*; the served register nearest today (a Beninese ministry chief of staff, 11 Feb 2026, reported) says results are *announced for the last quarter of 2026*. The piece carries the served date as announced, never as scheduled, and states the slip only if the slip is the story. The search tool's summary — "a launch in October 2026 in Dakar" — is not a served register (#41) and does not ride.
3. **When the ruler is about to be re-issued, the piece carries its own expiry.** #21 says a figure carries its vintage; this says a *ruler* does too. A piece built on PASEC 2019 in September 2026 states in-sentence that 2019 is the latest published cycle and that the next is announced for Q4 2026 — and names its own sequel. A crown handed out without saying the ruler is about to change is the aggregator's error (#29) in slow motion.

## Why it is #38 turned outward by one step

#38 said a document can disagree with itself and a figure carries its page. #43 says an *owner* can disagree with itself across two registers — the room and the page — and a figure carries its *register*. The hierarchy the Researcher applies, stated once: **the owner's published document > the owner's published data tables > the owner's first-party written statement > the owner's reported speech > any mirror.** Never average across the ranks; when the top rank cannot be read, drop one rank and say so.

## The Verifier's trace row (binding from this ruling)

For every figure attributed to an institution, the trace list records **which register of that institution** the figure was read in — *document / table / written statement / reported speech* — and the Verifier fails any figure whose only warrant is reported speech when the same owner has a published document on the same number. "The department said" is not a source line; "the department's report, p. N" is.
