## What the PISA 2025 notice gives

- **Jordan's three national averages, 2022 → 2025**: science 375 → 407, mathematics 361 → 388, reading
  342 → 363, described as statistically significant in all three.
- **Government-school averages, same two rounds**: 365 → 400, 353 → 382, 332 → 358.
- **Sample**: 8,290 students, 276 schools, across four educational authorities — the Ministry, private
  education, UNRWA and military culture.
- **Three named officials with their posts** (transliteration confirmed by the Arabic Editor 2026-11-26):
  **Dr Abdullah Ababneh**, president of NCHRD; **Dr Imad Ababneh**, national PISA coordinator;
  **Dr Azmi Mahafzah**, Minister of Education and Human Resources Development. *Two share a surname and are
  different people* — a piece naming either must name the given name and the post.
- **Two gaps reported as narrowed**, boys'/girls' schools and rural/badia schools, with **no direction given**.
- A press conference dated **Tuesday 8/9/2026** — the same day as the OECD's global launch. No venue, no
  convener, no rank printed anywhere in the notice.

## Cautions a drafter must carry

1. **The notice names the ministry in two forms** — *وزير التربية والتعليم والتنمية البشرية* in its first
   line and *وزارة التربية والتعليم وتنمية الموارد البشرية* thereafter. Reproduce each where it is printed.
2. **Its word for PISA's third domain is `القرائية`, literacy**, not `القراءة`. The English renders it
   *reading*, which is the domain's own name, and the annotation records the register's word.
3. **It calls PISA 2025 "the seventh round of the study" and does not say seventh of what.** Nothing read
   for the 11-26 piece settles whether that counts PISA's rounds or Jordan's participations.
4. **Everything in it is dated against 2022 and nothing against 2015 or earlier.** No Jordanian figure for
   any earlier round appears. The 2022 PDF above is the register for that, and it has not been read.
5. **Its comparative claims are its president's** — *among the few participating countries that recorded
   improvement in the three fields*, and *the largest among the participating Arab countries that have data
   available for comparison* — neither carrying a count, a list or a named comparison set, and neither
   addressed by any OECD register read so far.
6. **No archive holds a snapshot of the notice.** `qa_claim_registers` carries it `archive-unknown / not yet
   asked`, and under founder issue #12's applied default this operation may not send a new URL to an
   archive. **The piece's spine rests on one copy of one page**, named on that issue and in the Verifier's
   verdict. Re-read the register before quoting it again (#41), because that is the only control available.

## What the OECD's own registers say about Jordan

**One sentence, in one register.** Jordan is named only on the Volume I full-report page's since-2022
science improver list, and nowhere in the press release or the launch speech. It is on neither ten-year
list. Silence about mathematics and reading is silence, not contradiction — all three OECD lists are about
science (`content-drafts/recon/2026-11-26-ed06-pisa-2025-first-party-read.md`).
