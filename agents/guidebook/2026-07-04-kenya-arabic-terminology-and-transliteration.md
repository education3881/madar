# Style ruling — Kenyan (anglophone-Africa) proper nouns in Arabic + the تنسيق register

**Date:** 2026-07-04 · **Arabic Editor** (ruled at the Kenya CBE Grade-10 ship) · **Ruling #15** in the style/transliteration series (joins Vietnamese 06-16, tashkeel dek-cap 06-17)

## The register ruling (the durable part)

**التنسيق is the house term for centralised student *placement*; التوجيه is reserved for the *tracking debate*.** Egypt's admissions تنسيق is the pan-Arab reader's native mental model for "a central score places the student" — using it for Kenya's Grade-10 exercise makes the mechanism instantly legible to the Arabic reader in a way "placement" never is to the English one. But التوجيه (streaming/orientation, as in Tunisia/Morocco's توجيه) carries the *judgment* connotation — which direction a child is pointed — so the debate about early sorting is التوجيه while the administrative act is التنسيق. Keeping the two apart in Arabic preserves a distinction English blurs inside the single word "tracking," and it is *sharper* in Arabic than in the source language — bank it as a worked example that AR composition can out-precise the EN, not just match it (the 06-10 Iqra diglossia piece was the first; this is the second).

## Numbers

Western digits with the Arabic percent sign (٪) throughout, per the Brazil precedent; large figures keep the comma grouping (1,130,459) — legibility beats orthographic purism in data-bearing prose.
