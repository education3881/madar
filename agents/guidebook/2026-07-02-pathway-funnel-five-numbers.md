# Ruling #14 — The pathway funnel: five numbers, never one (2026-07-02)

**Trigger:** Kenya CBE Grade-10 recon. The same 1.13-million cohort carries four different "STEM numbers" in circulation at once — the policy **target** (60%), the assessed **potential** (KNEC: 59.09% "demonstrated potential to pursue STEM"), the learners' **choice** (>600,000 chose it, ~53%), and the actual **placement** (~50% placed) — and a fifth is coming: **arrival/provision** (how many sit in a STEM classroom that has a STEM teacher; TSC flags a 58,590-teacher deficit).

**The ruling.** Whenever a system reports tracking/pathway/stream figures, identify **which stage of the funnel** the number measures before using it:

1. **Target** — what policy wants (aspiration, set before any learner is tested)
2. **Potential** — what the assessment says learners could do (instrument-dependent)
3. **Choice** — what learners/families asked for (demand)
4. **Placement** — what the algorithm/authority assigned (supply meets demand under constraints)
5. **Arrival / provision** — what is actually taught and attended after reporting day

Different bodies own different stages (ministry owns 1 and 4; examinations council owns 2; families own 3; the schools and the teacher-service commission own 5), so the stages are **not reconcilable into one figure and must never be summed, averaged, or swapped silently**. The gaps *between* stages are usually the story: potential→choice is aspiration meeting information; choice→placement is capacity and algorithm design; placement→arrival is provision. A piece that quotes "X% are in STEM" without naming the stage has not cleared Test 1.

**Relationship to existing rulings:** extends #4 (measured-vs-announced) from *reporting body* to *funnel stage*; complements #12/#12b (administration and coverage axes) — a single blended score can also mix axes *within* one stage, as Kenya's placement score does (60% external KJSEA + 20% school-scored SBA + 20% KPSEA).

**Reusable check (compose-time):** for every pathway figure in a draft, the margin note must answer: *whose number, which stage, measured or announced?*
