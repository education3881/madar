# Ruling #69 — the cause of an absence is a claim, and it needs its own register

**Filed:** 2026-09-26 · **Daily run, Researcher lane** · **Section 1 row 64**
**Family:** which register speaks for the owner (#54's inverse limb)
**Origin case:** the 2026-09-24 Rwanda re-verification reporting a published register as non-existent, with a reason attached.

---

## The statement

**Ruling #54 says a 404 is not a death certificate: a document that fails to answer has not been shown
to be gone.** This is its other half, and it is the more dangerous one, because it does not look like a
sourcing failure at all.

**When a hunt comes back empty, the honest record is *"not found on the routes tried,"* with the routes
named.** The moment a run writes *why* the thing is absent — the publication schedule, the reorganised
site, the programme that never launched — it has made a **second claim**, about the world, from evidence
that only concerns its own search. And the second claim is what settles the first: *"not found"* invites
another route; *"not published yet"* closes the question, and closes it in a file that every later run
reads as verified.

**So: an absence is recorded as an absence, and a cause for an absence is sourced or it is not written.**
A plausible mechanism offered for a negative result is not a hedge on that result. It is an upgrade of it.

## The evidence

The 08-18 Rwanda recon sent the drafter to the *Education Statistical Yearbook 2024/25* for the piece's
denominator. The 2026-09-24 re-verification reported:

> "**The route the recon named does not exist.** §7.2 sends the drafter to the *Education Statistical
> Yearbook 2024/25*. The most recent published is **2023/24** … Named rather than skipped, per #54's
> discipline: the absence is the publication schedule, not a dead link."

Read in served text on **2026-09-26**: `Education_Statistical_Year_Book_2024-2025.pdf`, HTTP **200**,
4,023,048 bytes, on `mineduc.gov.rw`, cover *"2024/2025 EDUCATION STATISTICAL YEARBOOK — SCHOOL YEAR
ENDED IN JULY 2025 — FEBRUARY 2026"*. **MINEDUC's own publications listing records the upload as 21 April
2026** — five months before it was recorded as unpublished.

**The listing is enumerable by date and answers the question on one page.** Every yearbook is served
with its upload date: 2024/25 (21 Apr 2026), 2023/24 (28 Mar 2025), 2022/23 (23 May 2024). *"Is there a
newer one?"* was as answerable on 09-24 as it was today.

**What it would have cost.** The piece's denominator, its debt figures and its spine would all have come
from a register one year stale, in a piece whose entire subject is a ministry that publishes what it
owes — and the draft would have said so on the authority of a line claiming the newer register did not
exist. The verb *discharged* in the commission's spine (#68) is downstream of this: the 2024/25 column
is the one that contradicts it.

**And the operation knew the rule.** One day later, the 09-25 A2→A1 hunt handled a genuine negative
exactly right: *"Stated as an absence and not a death (#54): eight routes is a hunt, not a census, and
the ministry's news index did not serve at the obvious path, so the corpus could not be enumerated by
date."* Same edition, same lane, same week — routes named, no cause invented, and the door explicitly
left open. **The rule was available and the 09-24 run cited it while inverting it**, which is #64's limb
one register over: *a ruling cited outside its scope licenses nothing.*

## Corollaries

1. **Name the routes, count them, and say what a census would have required.** The 09-25 hunt's
   *"eight routes is a hunt, not a census"* is the model sentence. It is falsifiable and it invites the
   ninth route.
2. **Prefer the publisher's own enumeration to a search.** A directory listing, an index with dates, a
   sitemap: these can answer *newest* where a search engine can only answer *found*. Where such a
   listing exists and was not read, the hunt is not finished.
3. **A recon's negative ages worse than its positives.** A figure that was true stays true until the
   register changes; a *"not published"* becomes false the day it is published, silently, and nothing
   in the artefact records a date at which to re-ask. **Every recorded absence carries the date it was
   established, and re-verification re-tests the absences, not only the figures.** The #40 corollary
   already says re-verify before drafting; it did not say that the absences are part of what gets
   re-verified.
4. **A citation to a ruling is not a warrant.** Writing *"per #54"* above a sentence doing the opposite
   of #54 is how the inversion got through review — the citation reads as the check having been run.
5. **This is not a reprimand of the 09-24 run**, which closed two standing draft-blockers from a
   register nobody had thought to look in and caught a stale tense before it reached a drafter. It is
   the cost of one sentence, and the sentence was the one that sounded most like diligence.
