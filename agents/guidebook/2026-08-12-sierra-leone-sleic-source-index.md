## 2. Method note — **an equivalent-years (or percentile) claim carries its ruler** (fourth shape of the scoped-instrument family)
Row 15: a pledge is a scoped instrument. Row 19: a multiplier without a base. Row 20: a deficit is a ratio. Today's shape: **"X years of schooling" and "better than Y% of programmes" are conversions, and a conversion names its ruler or it isn't a figure.** Save the Children computed its math gain two ways: the standard LMIC ruler (0.15–0.21 SD ≈ one school year, Evans & Yuan) yields "1.1 to 1.6 years of schooling"; its own local, numeracy-specific Grade Effect (.359 SD per grade, from its own November-2022 ASER baseline) yields "two-thirds of a year." **It published the smaller number and said why** (local, subject-specific). Binding discipline for the draft: (a) every equivalent-years or percentile figure carries its ruler in the same sentence; (b) two conversions of the same SD are not two results — never quote "70% of programmes globally" (program-wide, EOF) beside "80th–90th percentile" (one provider, math only, Evans & Yuan distribution) as if they measure the same thing (#22); (c) **the choice of ruler is itself evidence** — a measured party that picks the harsher ruler on itself earns the reader's trust, and a report that picks the flattering one owes the reader the other number; (d) a gain that is not statistically significant is carried as *no demonstrated gain*, never as a small one.

## 3. Figure axes, Sierra Leone instance
- **Participants drift by register (#13/#21):** 134,000 (design, launch-era) → "more than 130,000" (funder, Jun 2026) → "more than 100,000" (completion register). Targeted ≠ reached ≠ verified-reached; the OPM report arbitrates. The drift is itself the story's asset: an outcomes contract is one of the few instruments where the reached number gets *audited down*.
- **The payment is the verdict:** behind-target → partial payment → budget not recouped. The instrument's integrity lives in the payment event, not the press release; the contract paying *less* is the system working.
- **Both directions of the gender texture named or neither:** the Y2 girls-ahead gap narrowed partly because boys rose (math) and partly because girls' gains fell (math).
- **Backdrop discipline:** English fell in treatment AND control (WASSCE-validated national decline); SLEIC math finished above baseline while control math finished flat. "Improved against a falling tide" is the honest positive.
