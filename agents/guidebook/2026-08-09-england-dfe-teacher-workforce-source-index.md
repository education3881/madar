## The corpus, by register (registers-by-load method)
- **gov.uk press releases (political register).** The claim of record for pledge progress — e.g. "Government hits 70% of pledge" (4 Jun 2026): 4,654/6,500, the "rebalancing" framing, the policy list. Serves clean text through our fetch path (confirmed 2026-08-09). Quote the government's word as the government's word ("on track," "record") — attribute, never adopt.
- **explore-education-statistics.service.gov.uk (statistical register).** Home of the two census instruments: **School Workforce in England** (Nov census: FTE totals, leavers, pay medians) and the **ITT census** (Dec: recruitment vs target by phase/subject). **Dashboard-first platform → #26 discipline:** headline "facts and figures" pages serve HTML text and are quotable; explorer tables render client-side and are *observations*, not citations. Every press-release figure has (or fails to have) its measured home here — the #18 measured-vs-announced check is one link deep.
- **NFER Teacher Labour Market in England, annual (analytic register).** Eighth report Mar 2026. The independent read: targets met/missed, *forecasts* (carry the word in-sentence), leaver **rates** (the government release speaks in leaver **counts** — never chain the two), and the attribution sentence (2026: improvement "likely boosted by weaker job prospects in the wider labour market" — the rival explanation to the policy list).
- **Hansard / written statements (statutory register).** Pay awards: HCWS171 (1 Jul 2026) — STRB 2026/27 recommendation 3.5% accepted in full, +3% from Sept 2027 (multi-year). Clean served text, low fetch risk.
- **Full Fact + Schools Week (watchdog register).** The *timeline of the definition*: manifesto wording (Jul 2024) → Feb 2026 scope confirmation (secondary + special + colleges, baseline 2023/24, net growth). Good for chronology; never citation-of-record for figures.

## Method ruling filed with this row — *a pledge is a scoped instrument*
A political pledge, once measured, behaves exactly like an assessment instrument: it has a **scope** (#22), a **baseline vintage** (#21), and a **population** (#25). When a government reports progress against a pledge, the citable unit is the *pair*: the progress figure **and** the scope-as-defined-on-a-date. England, summer 2026, is the clean specimen: **+4,654** (inside the Feb-2026 scope: secondary + special + FE vs 2023/24) and **−1,900** (whole-workforce FTE, Nov 2025 y/y) are simultaneously true; quoting either without the other's existence is the error. Generalises directly to any target-carrying reform we cover (Egypt's hiring drive, Gulf nationalization quotas, India's vacancy commitments): **first question at recon — "more of *what*, counted against *which baseline*, in *which scope*, defined *when*?"** Extends #18 (measured-vs-announced) and the seven-axis figure family; adds nothing numbered — the axes already cover it, this row names the *application to workforce counts*.
