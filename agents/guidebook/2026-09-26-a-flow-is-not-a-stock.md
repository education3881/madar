# Ruling #68 — a flow is not a stock

**Filed:** 2026-09-26 · **Daily run, Researcher lane** · **Section 1 row 63**
**Family:** figure integrity — a new axis beside the seven (and #14's nearest relative)
**Origin case:** Edition 05 row 6 (Rwanda) — 24,000 teachers certified against 27,409 counted untrained.

---

## The statement

**Ruling #14 says *entered ≠ trained ≠ sat ≠ passed ≠ certified* — five stages of one funnel, and a
figure must say which stage it is.** Every one of those five is the same *kind* of quantity: a count of
people who passed a point. This ruling is the axis #14 does not cover.

**A count of events over a period and a count of a population at an instant are different kinds of
number, and no arithmetic relates them without a third quantity nobody published.** A programme's
output is a **flow**: certifications issued, teachers trained, places filled, between two dates. A
workforce's composition is a **stock**: how many untrained teachers are standing in classrooms on
census day. Subtracting a flow from a stock assumes the stock's only movement was the flow — that
nobody arrived, nobody left, nobody was hired untrained while the programme ran. **That assumption is
almost never true and is almost never stated.**

**The tell is that the subtraction feels like arithmetic rather than like a claim.** 27,322 untrained,
24,000 certified, therefore ~3,300 remain — it reads as a calculation. It is a forecast, and the thing
it forecasts is the arrival rate, which is exactly what neither register measured.

## The evidence

Rwanda opened a certification programme in **April 2024** and the World Bank and REB reported in **March
2026** that **24,000** teachers had been trained and certified. MINEDUC's own Education Statistical
Yearbooks count untrained teaching staff at each census:

| | 2023/24 (year ended July 2024) | 2024/25 (year ended July 2025) |
|---|---|---|
| Untrained pre-primary | 5,162 (47.4%) | **5,692 (44.2%)** |
| Untrained primary | 22,160 (32.4%) | **21,717 (30.2%)** |
| **Combined** | 27,322 | **27,409** |
| Pre-primary + primary workforce | 79,224 | **84,763** |

**The stock went up while 24,000 certificates were being issued.** Primary untrained fell by 443;
pre-primary untrained rose by 530. The ministry's own word for the primary movement is **"marginally."**
Trained primary teachers rose by 3,996 (46,166 → 50,162) — the programme is visible in the register, and
it is visible on the *trained* line, not on the untrained one.

**And the ministry names the missing quantity itself.** The persistence is attributed *"particularly to
newly recruited primary school teachers."* The workforce grew by **5,539** in one year. That is the
arrival rate, in the subject's own voice, and it is what the subtraction would have had to assume away.

**Neither register is wrong and neither is hiding anything.** A subtraction between them would have been
the only false sentence in the set, and it would have been ours.

## Corollaries

1. **A flow and a stock may be juxtaposed and must not be netted.** Where the two belong in the same
   passage — and here they are the finding — the passage says they are different kinds of quantity and
   names the movement that separates them.
2. **Ask which quantity is missing, and whether the subject published it.** For flow-against-stock the
   missing quantity is always arrivals-and-departures over the same window. Here the subject published
   it. Often it does not, and then the two numbers simply cannot be related in print.
3. **A programme's own headline is a flow by construction.** *N trained*, *N certified*, *N enrolled*,
   *N reached* — these are what an implementer can count, and an implementer reporting a flow is not
   claiming anything about a stock. Do not read the claim in for them.
4. **A share needs both quantities from the same side of the divide.** "24,000 out of a workforce of
   84,763" is a flow over a stock and is not a percentage of anything; "21,717 of 71,879 primary
   teachers are untrained, 30.2%" is the register's own share and is sound.
5. **Direction is not a synonym for progress.** A falling *share* (32.4% → 30.2%) with a nearly flat
   *count* means the denominator grew. Both are true, they answer different questions, and a piece that
   carries only the share has reported a hiring rate as an improvement.
6. **The positive posture survives this.** What is admirable here is not that the debt was retired — it
   was not — but that the debtor published the number showing it had not been, in its own statistical
   annual, and named its own hiring as the cause. That is the same virtue this publication credited
   Brazil with, and it is a harder one than the discharge would have been.

## Where it bit before anyone noticed

The commission of 2026-09-25 wrote the spine as *"ran a weekend-and-holiday certification programme
that **discharged most of that debt**."* No figure in it was wrong. The verb was, and it was licensed by
a subtraction nobody performed on paper — the two numbers were simply near each other, and *most* is
what a reader computes when a flow and a stock are put side by side without their kinds named.

**Caught because the 2026-09-13 rule made the drafter open the register instead of the annotation**,
and the register that opened was one newer than the recon knew about (#69). Three rulings had to be in
force for this to be caught before drafting rather than after banking.
