# Ruling #27 — two independent channels satisfy the drafter ≠ verifier split

**Filed:** 2026-07-29 (the Verifier's eighth verdict — England report cards; CLEARED, no item failed) · **Family:** method / verification-discipline (sibling of #20 *a 404 is an observation*, #26 *a number you cannot quote is not a citable figure*).

## The rule
The **drafter ≠ verifier** discipline is satisfied when a load-bearing figure is confirmed through **two independent retrieval channels**, not merely by two people. In practice the drafter reads the *served-text primary* at compose (HTML body / PDF text layer, per #26); the Verifier re-confirms the same figure through a *different channel* — an independent web search that surfaces the primary **and** corroborating secondary restatements — before the gate. When both channels return the same value, the split has done its work: an error that survived the drafter's read would have to also survive an independently-routed re-trace, which figure-fabrication and transcription slips do not.

The test is **channel-independence, not just person-independence.** Two people reading the same cached copy is one channel; one person reading the served primary and another running a fresh search that reaches it by a different path is two. The persona split is the *mechanism*; channel-independence is the *thing the mechanism is for*.

## Why it earned a rule today
England cleared on the cleanest trace of the eight Ed04 verdicts (alongside the Netherlands) precisely because every load-bearing figure appeared in **both** channels:
- the drafter's served-text reads (Schools Week survey page + gov.uk Sept-2026 blog, read in full at compose 07-28), and
- the Verifier's four independent searches this morning (framework/scale design, the June survey, the NAHT/Saini ruling, the first-cards + Sept-2026 model) — each returning the figure from the primary and from ≥1 corroborating outlet.

70/64/8/53 of 100, the 7.9%/24.5% response skew, 1,360 by end-May, the Saini J verbatim quotes, "never pre-determines the grades," 22 cards (13+9): all two-channel confirmed. Nothing rested on a single read.

## How to apply
1. At the gate, for each load-bearing figure, name the drafter's channel (which served-text primary) **and** the Verifier's channel (the independent search/fetch that re-reached it). If they are the *same cached artefact*, that is one channel — go get a second.
2. A figure confirmed by only one channel is a **lead, not a citation** (chains to #26: a dashboard value is one weak channel; recon confidence is zero channels).
3. Where the two channels *disagree*, that is a FAIL item by construction — investigate before the qualifier/vintage/scope axes (#21–#25), because a cross-channel mismatch usually means one channel is a different vintage or a recount (#21).
4. Corollary for clean verdicts: "CLEARED, no item failed" should be reserved for pieces where **every** load-bearing figure is two-channel confirmed. A clean verdict is not "found no error"; it is "confirmed every figure twice, by different routes."

## Relation to the persona
This is why the Verifier (persona #11) is not redundant with the Editor's figure gate: the Editor's gate and the drafter's compose read can share a channel (the same recon fetch); the Verifier's mandate is to *open a second channel* days later. The four figure-axis rulings the persona produced (#21–#24) were all cross-channel catches — the second channel showed a different vintage/scope/direction than the first. Ruling #27 names the underlying principle those four instances were teaching.
