# Ruling #61 — Three reproductions are three compressions

**Filed:** 2026-09-22 (Editor's five-test pair verdict, Ed05 row 5, Egypt)
**Family:** Sourcing discipline / non-numeric — with #41, #43, #51, and the
2026-09-13 *annotation == sentence* rule
**Status:** Binding.

---

## The ruling

**When a primary document is read through reproductions, the reproductions do not
differ only in which one you can reach. They differ in how much of the clause each
one keeps — and a guardrail that records one axis of their disagreement certifies
nothing about the others.**

Where a piece cites a statute, a report or a judgment through second-hand
reproductions, the clause is taken from the **fullest** of them, that one is named
in the annotation as the one it was taken from, and the ways the others compress
it are recorded rather than averaged away.

## Origin

`2026-09-19-egypt-baccalaureate-published-first` read Law 169 of 2025 on three
served Arabic reproductions: a legal encyclopaedia carrying the full enacted text,
a law practice's article-by-article commentary, and a Youm7 news write-up quoting
the articles. The draft recorded a guardrail about them, and it was a real one:
*the reproductions disagree about which numbered bis-article carries the fee
provision, so the provisions are cited by content and never by sub-article
number.* That guardrail rode into both bodies and both `sources[]` blocks.

It was about the **numbering**. They also disagreed about the **text**, and
nothing looked.

The statute's fee clause, in Article 37 *mukarrar* 2, has two halves:

> ويكون التقدم للامتحان المرة الأولى مجانًا، ويحدد بقرار من وزير التربية والتعليم
> والتعليم الفنى فئات رسوم التقدم للامتحان للمرات التالية بما لا يجاوز **مائتي جنيه
> للمادة الواحدة فى المرة الواحدة**، **ولوزير التربية والتعليم والتعليم الفنى بعد
> موافقة مجلس الوزراء أن يُصدر قرارًا بزيادة هذا الحد تدريجيًا**، على ألا يتجاوز
> مجموع الرسوم أربعمائة جنيه للمادة الواحدة.

Two hundred pounds **per subject per attempt**; and, above it, a **permission** —
the Minister *may*, with Cabinet approval, raise that ceiling gradually, to a total
not exceeding four hundred pounds per subject.

The piece printed: *"a retake bounded at two hundred Egyptian pounds per attempt
and not exceeding four hundred per subject."* The unit lost *per subject*. The
permission became a price. Both languages, identically, **and both annotations,
identically** — so the `annotation == sentence` hand-off line could not have caught
it, the second instance of that shape in this edition (slot 3, item 2, 09-17).

Where each half came from is the finding:

| | the 200 | the 400 | the escalation clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Source 1** — legal encyclopaedia | *per subject per attempt* | present | **present, in full** |
| **Source 3** — Youm7 | *per attempt* only | present | **present, in full** |
| **Source 2** — law practice | quotation **truncated at 200 with an ellipsis** | only in its own plain-language summary and its reader FAQ | **absent** |

The piece's unit came from source 3. Its ceiling came from source 2's *summary* —
a numeral pair the practice wrote **beside** the text it was summarising, never
inside its quotation of it. The one reproduction that carried the whole clause was
read on the same day, on the same screen, and supplied neither half.

## Corollaries

1. **A gloss printed beside a text is the text you will quote.** A summary is
   shaped like a sentence; a statute is not. When a register serves both, the
   summary is what the hand reaches for, and #43's hierarchy applies *inside a
   single document*: the «النص» block outranks the «الشرح المبسط» beside it.
2. **The number was never in doubt; the clause around it was.** Every numeric
   instrument this operation owns would pass this piece — 400 is in the register,
   twice. It is a bound on a decree nobody has issued. The failure is #51's shape
   with the scope in the *modality* rather than in the set.
3. **A recorded disagreement is not a check.** Having written down that the
   reproductions disagree about numbering, the draft felt audited. The
   guardrail's own existence is what made the second disagreement invisible:
   *this has been looked at* is the strongest possible camouflage for the part
   that has not.
4. **Compression is directional and asymmetric.** A press reproduction drops
   qualifying units. A practitioner's summary drops conditionals. An archive drops
   nothing and may be unreachable. The compressions are predictable enough to look
   for by name.

## Binding

1. Where a document is read through more than one reproduction, the piece names
   **which one each load-bearing clause was taken from**, and it is the fullest
   one that can be reached.
2. Where a reproduction prints both a text and a summary, only the text is
   quotable. A figure that appears **only** in the summary does not ride.
3. Where reproductions are recorded as disagreeing on any axis, the clause is
   compared across all of them **word for word** before the pair verdict — the
   recorded disagreement is the signal to compare, not a substitute for it.
4. A statutory maximum that exists only if an authority acts is reported as a
   permission, with the actor and the condition in the same sentence as the
   number.

*Also found in the same pass, and recorded here because they belong to the same
reading:* a quotation carried as *"not less than two hundred pounds"* where the
register serves *"shall not be less than…"* (#46, the unmarked emendation, second
instance after Sierra Leone 09-09); and a `sources[]` entry naming a headline the
document does not carry — *'Egypt's parliament approves optional baccalaureate
system'* against the served *'Parliament approves new education bill amid
inequality fears'*. **A citation's title is part of the citation.**
