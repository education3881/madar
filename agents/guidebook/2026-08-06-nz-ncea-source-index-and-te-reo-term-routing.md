## Part A — Source index: the NZ co-requisite corpus (splits by institution-job)

The New Zealand assessment corpus is primary-source-rich and splits cleanly by **which body owns which claim** — the same institution-job discipline banked for Chile (Mineduc vs Agencia, 2026-08-05). Route each figure to the body that is its system of record:

- **NZQA (the qualifications authority)** owns the *canonical numbers*. Two registers:
  - `www2.nzqa.govt.nz/ncea/subjects/litnum/literacy-and-numeracy-data/` — the per-round results tables (the standalone-assessment pass rates by round; the **only** home of the May-2026 Event-1 series — reading 60.5 / writing 56.8 / numeracy 51.7, >82,900 sitting). This is the series of record; the ministry's recount does **not** overwrite it (see #21).
  - `www2.nzqa.govt.nz/about-us/news/ncea-and-university-entrance-attainment-in-2025/` (27 Mar 2026) — the co-requisite-by-end-of-Year-11 attainment (69.5 → 73.8), the Jann Marshall quote, and the equity-disparity finding in NZQA's own words.
- **Beehive.govt.nz (the government's release channel)** owns the *political framing + the funding line*: the 14 Jul 2025 "surge" release (May-2025 vs May-2024 round; lower-decile 19.8 → 34; NZ$2.2m to 141 schools; **the 71.5 → 79.6 recount** — carried here as a ministry recount, not an NZQA revision) and the 16 May 2026 "replacement confirmed" release (Foundational Award 2028 / NZ Certificate 2029 / Advanced 2030, A+–E, all *announced not operating*). **Beehive is Incapsula-walled to datacenter egress** (banked 07-13) — reach it via **Scoop full-text mirror** per #20; the figures are restated in NZQA registers for cross-check.
- **ncea.education.govt.nz** owns the *instrument definition*: the 20-credit rule (10 literacy/te reo matatini + 10 numeracy/pāngarau), mandatory-from-2024, Common Assessment Activities twice a year; and the transition-standards pathway running to end-2027.
- **RNZ** carries the ERO evaluation as received ("pass mark from principals," Nov 2024) — a secondary restatement, fine for the qualitative claim, not for a figure (#20: secondary restatements carry figures, not sentences).

**Two-base trap (carried in-sentence per #19, an instance of #25's conditional-rate rule):** Level-1 pass rates count **only entrants** (~⅓ of Year 11 skip optional Level 1); co-requisite rates count **the whole cohort** by end of Year 11. The two bases must never share a sentence — Marshall's own "amongst participating students" is the tell. Verified 2026-07-21 (scoped re-check CLEARED).

**Vintage trap (#21, first-caught here):** the 2024 Level-1 rate is **71.5** in NZQA's standing annual series; **79.6** is the ministry's mid-2025 recount of the same cohort with late co-requisite clearances added. "A recount is not a revision" — attribute the recount to its author + vintage; carry the series figure alongside.

## Part B — Transliteration routing: te reo Māori content-terms (extends #28b)

**The rule.** A source-language **content-term** (not an institutional acronym) is routed to Arabic by **its own language's phonology**, kept with its **Latin tag in parentheses + a one-time Arabic gloss** — the same dual-surface treatment #28a gives institutional acronyms, but the transliteration itself follows the term's *origin language*, not the surrounding English. This is #28b ("route each name-part by its own origin") generalised from **person-name parts** to **embedded content-terms**.

**Applied (nz-ncea):**
- **te reo matatini** → «تي ريو ماتاتيني» (te reo matatini) — Māori: *te reo* = "the language," *matatini* = literacy/multiliteracy. Transliterated by Māori values (macron-free, each vowel voiced), **not** anglicised.
- **pāngarau** → «بانغاراو» (pāngarau) — Māori for numeracy/mathematics. The macron `ā` marks vowel length, not a new letter → rendered `ا`; `ng` is the single Māori velar-nasal digraph → `نغ`. Arabic keeps `ب` (MSA convention; `پ` avoided).
- Both glossed once in-prose ("or te reo matatini for students assessed in te reo Māori"), then usable bare.

**Why routing matters.** The same string can carry two systems in one sentence — a Māori term inside an English institutional name (`te reo matatini` credits within `NCEA`). Anglicising the Māori term (e.g. reading `pāngarau` through English stress) would misvoice it exactly as routing a Spanish surname's `‑z` through Italian misvoiced would (the Chile Cortez case, #28b). Route the Māori term by Māori, the acronym by #28a, in the same clause.

**Named-human gate (this piece):** **Jann Marshall → جان مارشال** CONFIRMED-by-convention (both parts standard Anglo Arabic-media forms; not "unheard" per #28b, contrast Australia's *Gniel* PROVISIONAL). NCEA / NZQA / ERO / CAA each keep the Latin tag + Arabic gloss per #28a.

**Generalises to:** any indigenous- or minority-language content-term embedded in a majority-language education system — Māori (NZ), te reo cognates, and by extension pāngarau-class terms in future Pacific/First-Nations pieces. Route by origin; tag + gloss once; never anglicise-then-transliterate.
