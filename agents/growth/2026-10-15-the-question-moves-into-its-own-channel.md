## 2 · The action: the blocker moves into the founder-decision channel — **issue #11**

The packet has been post-ready since **2026-08-31**, its step 1 has been whole since **2026-10-13**, the
Arabic Editor's gate on all five tag sets approves, and nothing editorial is outstanding. The only thing
holding it is that this repository has no posting credential and should not have one.

**Four consecutive notes said exactly that and carried the question to the weekly review. That is not
asking it.** The skill is explicit about the shape a founder-owned call takes — a GitHub issue with the
options, their consequences, a recommendation, **a stated default and a stated date** — and about what to
do afterwards: carry on with the default, and say in the brief that it was applied. Five notes of correct
diagnosis never once used that channel. **The defect was not the diagnosis; it was the venue.**

**Issue #11 is open**, labelled `founder-decision`, carrying:

- **A** — Instagram opening grid: reach becomes possible, return rate still does not, recurring action required.
- **B** — Substack Issue 01: one action, and the **only option that turns on a number we are permitted to report.**
- **C** — stop producing packets for channels that do not exist; redirect growth to owned surfaces (feeds, internal graph, discoverability, source health).
- **Recommendation: B, then C as the standing shape.**
- **Default if no reply by 2026-10-22: C** — the packet and the 25 files in `social-drafts/` are marked
  **parked** rather than left looking pending, and the brief records that a default was applied.

**Why a default of C rather than "keep waiting":** the cost of waiting is not zero and is now visible.
`social-drafts/` holds **25 files**; the newest send-ready packet was written on **2026-08-31** and the
growth function has produced no new packet since, because producing a sixth artefact for a channel with no
send step is not an audience action. Parking the backlog in writing is honest; leaving it to look pending
is the same defect as a count nobody maintains.
