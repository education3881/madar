# Growth, 2026-11-27 — the Edition 06 cluster, closed and the hole that made it named

**The action:** every Edition 06 piece now reaches every other Edition 06 piece in **one hop**, in both
languages. **Ordered pairs with no direct rail: 3 → 0 of 12.**

**Why this is a growth action and not housekeeping.** Edition 06 is four pieces on one release, published
across two days, and the reader who arrives on any one of them arrives from a search engine or a share, not
from the front page. The rails are the only path from the piece they landed on to the other three. The
cluster was *undirected*-connected before this — a reader could get anywhere eventually — but three ordered
pairs needed two hops, and two of those three had the newest and most load-bearing piece of the edition on
the far side.

**The outcome is readable without anyone's permission**, which is the standing preference for a bet: it is a
property of files in this repository, measured by a dozen lines that read the content collection, and it
does not depend on a tracker, a platform or a third party telling us anything.

## What the measurement found, which is worth more than the repair

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| Edition 06 pieces | 4 | 4 |
| ordered pairs with a direct rail | 9 of 12 | **12 of 12** |
| undirected components | 1 | 1 |
| EN and AR rail lists identical | yes | yes |
| any piece over the 6-rail cap | no | no (max 5) |

**Two of the three missing pairs had one cause, and it is structural rather than careless.** The rail step
added on 2026-11-12 says cell 1 names the forward edges a piece owes and cell 8 writes them. **Cell 1 cannot
name a piece that does not exist yet.** `2026-11-26-qatar-pisa-2025-two-of-three` was commissioned on 11-26
and shipped on 11-27, and `2026-11-27-thailand-pisa-2025-fourth-list` shipped in between. Qatar's own
`related:` points at Thailand — the drafter added it, correctly, because by then Thailand existed — but the
*forward* edge back into Thailand was never owed, because the list of owed edges had been frozen a day
earlier.

Under the founder directive something ships **every day**, so a piece whose gate spans even one day is
commissioned against a board that has moved by the time it flips. That is now most pieces.

**The amendment is in the ledger's rail step, and it is one sentence:** at cell 8 the forward edges are
**derived from the piece's own final `related:`**, not read from cell 1's list. Cell 1's list stays as the
Editor's statement of intent and as the floor; it is no longer the specification.

*Standing-queue item 72 diagnosed this family exactly — "a rail is curated when a piece is written, and at
that moment only the older pieces exist, so every follow-up can point back and none can point forward". The
11-12 fix moved the curation from drafting time to cell 8 and then kept reading a list assembled at cell 1,
which carried the same horizon one step further along. A fix that relocates a decision without relocating
its inputs has moved the defect, not closed it.*

## The third pair was deliberate and is now not

`jordan-pisa-2025-counts-from-2022` ↔ `qatar-pisa-2025-two-of-three` had no rail in either direction because
Qatar's brief prohibits comparing Qatar with Jordan **across different lists**. Re-read today: the Qatar
piece prints Jordan's Table I.2.6 row inside a four-system table that is **one** list answering **one**
question, which is the arrangement the prohibition exists to protect rather than the one it forbids. The
rail is honest and both directions were added.

No change to the chrome, the front page or the Editions registry. Edition 06's registry row went in on
