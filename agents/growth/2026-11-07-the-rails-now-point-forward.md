## What the row asked, and what was actually left of it

Item 72 was raised on 2026-11-02 after the growth function turned three edges round: **138 directed
`related:` edges over the 44 approved pairs, 98 reciprocated, 40 one-way — and 34 of the 40 pointing
from a newer piece to an older one.** The row's diagnosis was a clock rather than a defect: *a rail is
curated when a piece is written, and at that moment only the older pieces exist, so every follow-up can
point back and none can point forward, and nothing ever goes back.* Its stated taking was two things —
a step in the commission template, and **one Editor pass over the remaining reverse edges to decide
which are warranted.**

Re-measured this morning before touching anything: **138 edges, 98 reciprocated, 40 one-way, 33 of them
backward in time, over 18 distinct older targets.** The row's shape held. One number in it did not
survive re-measurement and is corrected here: the row said *sixteen published pieces are pointed at by a
newer piece and offer no forward path*; under the strict reading — a piece with **no** rail to anything
newer than itself — the count today is **three**. The sixteen is the count of pieces with an
*unreciprocated* follower, which is 18 by today's reading. **Two different questions, and the row
carried one number for both.**

cross-system comparative, which is what this publication mostly writes, so *same country* could not do
the deciding and the only available test was whether the newer piece is on the **same question**.

**Twenty-two reverse edges were left alone, and that is the pass's actual output.** The commonest
reason: the newer piece cites the older one as *one of several comparators* and the older piece's

Leone teacher piece is not thereby owed a route to Vietnamese tuition policy. **A reciprocity sweep

not.

**Ceiling respected and not guessed:** the longest rail list in the served corpus was **6**
(`sierra-leone-tsc-teacher-matching`), so additions were capped so that no piece exceeds 6. Oman and
Korea and Zambia now sit at the proven maximum rather than past it.

## Why this is a growth action and not tidying

A rail is the only route this publication offers from one piece to another that does not pass through a
browse index. **The older piece is the likelier landing page** — it has had the longer accumulated
exposure, and three of the five pieces above are among the oldest on the site — and until this morning a
reader who arrived on Oman's ratings piece from anywhere had no path to four later pieces this
publication wrote on Oman's own question. That is a reader leaving because we did not tell them there
was more, which is the only growth mechanism this operation has: **being worth returning to.**

**And the outcome is readable without anybody's permission**, which the CHARTER requires of a bet: the
graph is in our own frontmatter, the counts above are re-countable by any later run, and nothing here
depends on a tracker we do not have and will not add. **No traffic figure is given and none is
available** — the site carries no third-party tracker by design.
