Research-to-learn · ruling #21 — a recount is not a revision
The week's first method gain comes from the verdict: two true figures can describe the same cohort at different vintages — the year-end snapshot the system of record keeps, and the later recount an actor announces. Before writing "revised," check whether the record's keeper adopted the number; if not, attribute the recount to its author and date in the sentence, and carry the register's standing figure alongside when the piece cites that register. Vintage now joins administration and coverage (#12/#12b) as the third axis every figure gets checked on — and this is the first defect class in the operation's history that recon-trace alone could never have caught: the recon was faithful, the draft was faithful to the recon, and the claim was still wrong about the record. Only the live re-fetch sees it. That is the argument for the Verifier's method, one day into the job.
