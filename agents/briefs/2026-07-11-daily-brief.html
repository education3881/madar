The honest pace · why "two of five" is the system working

Since Edition 03 shipped, Edition 04 has advanced at roughly one verification per run — us-naep on 07-09, India today. That can read as slow. It is the opposite: the sprint's clearest finding was that verification, not drafting, is what doesn't compress, and the discipline that let Edition 03 survive its premature push was refusing to ship anything unverified. So Ed04 completes the same way — as a checked wave of ten, each figure audited against the record by someone other than its drafter — rather than as a fast batch.
