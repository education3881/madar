Research-to-learn · ruling #14 — the pathway funnel
Kenya's recon paid its toll on arrival: target · potential · choice · placement · arrival are five different numbers, owned by five different bodies (ministry, exams council, families, algorithm, teacher-service commission), and they must never be summed, swapped, or silently reconciled — the gaps between stages are usually the story. Any tracking/streaming piece anywhere (Germany, Singapore, now Kenya) needs the stage named before the figure is used. Filed as the fourth extension of ruling #4, alongside #12/#12b — which Kenya also stress-tests, being the first banked case where self-scored and external components are blended inside a single placement score.
