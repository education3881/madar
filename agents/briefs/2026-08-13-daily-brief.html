Sudan built the classroom for this catastrophe a decade before it arrived.

Your second mandated Africa slot has its angle. A decade before Sudan's war closed half the country's schools, Sudan invented the classroom that doesn't need one — e-Learning Sudan, 2014: a mathematics game on a solar-charged tablet, run by a trained neighbour, in villages that had no school to close. It was trialled rigorously, scaled into the global Can't Wait to Learn platform that kept ~125,000 children learning through other countries' wars — and when Sudan's own war came, the no-school classroom came home: 699 safe learning spaces since the start of 2024, sixty of them digital, reaching 12,660+ displaced children across eight states. The accomplishment is an idea the war has not managed to kill. One honesty note first: yesterday's block never pushed — today's push carries two days.
