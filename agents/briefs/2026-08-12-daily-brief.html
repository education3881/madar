Research · guidebook row 21 — an equivalent-years claim carries its ruler
The scoped-instrument family gains its fourth shape: a pledge is scoped (row 15), a multiplier needs its base (row 19), a deficit is a ratio (row 20) — and now "X years of schooling" is a conversion, and a conversion names its ruler or it isn't a figure. The gift in today's sources: Save the Children computed its gain on two rulers and published the smaller one — its own local Grade Effect gives "two-thirds of a year," where the standard LMIC ruler would have let it claim 1.1–1.6 years. The banked rule: the choice of ruler is itself evidence — a measured party that picks the harsher ruler earns trust; a report that picks the flattering one owes the reader the other number.
