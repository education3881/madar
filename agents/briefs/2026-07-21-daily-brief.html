Research · ruling #24 — a series point carries its position
Putting a fresh data point into a running series has two traps that only column headers police. Position: compare same-position events (May against May), never the adjacent column — resitter and second-chance bases sit one column over and skew everything. Base note: each year's table travels with a cohort-composition note, and NZQA's two notes describe different denominators ("nearly half Year 10" covers the combined-event cohort; "around half Year 10" covers Event 1 only) — so the draft carries the cited point's note and asserts no cross-year sameness. Position is the sixth named axis a figure travels with, joining administration/coverage, funnel stage, vintage, scope and direction. Every edition we publish cites at least one recurring national series; the move is mechanical — put a finger on the column header of both numbers before comparing.
