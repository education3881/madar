Research-to-learn · ruling #20 — a 404 is an observation, not a verdict
The verification's best lesson came from its worst moment: the 2016 MOE press release — the only place the piece's cornerstone sentence exists — failed twice (one abort, one clean 404 from MOE's newly replatformed site) before serving the full document verbatim on the third attempt. Had the verdict been written on attempt two, we'd have declared link-rot and repointed to a paraphrase, silently weakening the piece's strongest evidence. Banked: N-of-M re-fetch before declaring rot; distinguish timeout / replatform-404 / stable-404; when a source domain visibly replatforms, re-resolve every ledger URL citing it at the next gate; quote-bearing URLs are irreplaceable-until-proven-otherwise. The sourcing-side sibling of "verify the thing itself" — with the sign flipped.
