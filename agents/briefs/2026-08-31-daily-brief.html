Research — ruling #40: a mirror can misname the instrument
A programme's name is a figure. It carries provenance like a number and is traced to the instrument's owner, never taken from a mirror's headline — the recon carried “BRIDGE” from a Tribune headline for eighteen days while every traced figure around it stayed clean, because values, vintages and scopes were axes and the name was not. Now it is: mirror-sourced names are PROVISIONAL until the owner serves them, and are never transliterated before then — a transliteration of a wrong name is two defects wearing one word.
