The recurring defect class

One pattern crossed the line from incident to class. The frontmatter dek cap (200 code points) was breached a third time on 07-22 (Netherlands, 250→200), after Korea (06-17) and Oman (06-20). Every instance was caught at the build/gate and fixed same-run — the backstop works — but every instance was first measured at the backstop, never at compose, despite a prevention rule codified for exactly this on 06-24. A defect that recurs after codification is not a knowledge gap; it is an execution gap.

Prevention rule codified into the RUNBOOK this week (enforcement addendum):

  • The draft hand-off to the Editor is incomplete without the recorded count — the drafter pastes dek: NNN/200 · title: NN/100 (code points, tashkeel +1 each) into the verdict-request. A hand-off missing that line is returned unread, not verified-then-flagged.
  • The Verifier's trace list carries a dek/title within cap at compose: ☐ row, failed if the count line is absent — an unmeasured cap is an open item, exactly like an untraced figure.

No near-miss reached production this fortnight; the held-draft flag-sweep caught its seventh latent approved:true on staged copies (07-17, twenty Ed03 duplicates) and defused all of them — the #18 family stays closed on disk.

3 — Knowledge Consolidation

Five rulings, one checklist

The daily runs filed cleanly all fortnight — every increment is already in the guidebook and named in the INDEX; a sweep confirmed zero un-indexed files and no duplicates. The consolidation value this week was synthesis, not cleanup.

The Verifier's five verdicts produced five rulings (#21–#25), each a new "axis a figure travels with." Scattered across five daily addenda, they were five things to remember; gathered, they are one mental model. I've consolidated them — with the earlier #12/#12b and #14 — into a single seven-axes figure-integrity checklist at the top of the guidebook INDEX:

#AxisThe question a number must answerRuling
1Administration / coverageWho ran it, over what population? (self-administered ≠ external; census ≠ sample)#12/#12b
2Funnel stageWhich stage — target, potential, choice, placed? Don't chain stages into a trend.#14
3VintageDid the system of record adopt it, or is it a later recount by another author?#21
4ScopeWhat does each qualifier govern? Don't split a shared one or narrow a broad one.#22
5DirectionTrace the hedge ("almost"/"over") to the same sentence that gave the value.#23
6PositionA series point carries its column and base note; never assert cross-year sameness.#24
7ConditionWrite "out of ___" for every rate; match the draft's population to the source's.#25

The unifying move — check at the context, not the figure — now sits in one place for the Verifier instead of seven.
