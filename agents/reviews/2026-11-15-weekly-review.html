Ruling #144 — a maintenance rule licenses the whole sentence

A sentence that carries its own maintenance rule is read as maintained in all of its claims, and the rule covers only the clause it was written for — so the un-ruled claims beside it decay faster than they would with no rule at all, because the rule is what a reader checks and its green answer is taken for the sentence’s.

The test is one question, asked of the rule rather than of the prose: what would make this rule fire? Everything in the sentence that cannot make it fire is unmaintained.
