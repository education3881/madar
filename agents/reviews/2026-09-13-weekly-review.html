The near-miss shape — and it reproduced

Two of the six are the identical defect, two days and two pieces and two drafters apart. In both, the hedge was carried correctly in sources[], in both languages — and dropped from both bodies. Sierra Leone cited its own source more carefully than it wrote itself; Sudan did the same thing with the laureate year, and the correct year was sitting in the annotation the whole time. A defect that reproduces across drafters is not a slip. The mechanism, once named, is obvious: the citation is composed with the register open, because it is a citation; the sentence is composed afterwards from memory of the citation, because it is prose — and the hedge, the noun, the date and the setting are exactly what memory smooths.

Codified into the RUNBOOK — rule 1 of 2

The annotation and the sentence are written from the same OPEN register.

(1) A load-bearing sentence is composed with its register open in front of the drafter, never from the annotation — if the annotation had to be consulted to write the sentence, the source is re-opened instead. (2) The hand-off to the Editor carries an annotation == sentence line per load-bearing source (hedge, noun, date, setting), and a hand-off missing it is returned unread — the same teeth the dek/title cap got on 2026-07-27, applied at the same ruler. (3) The Verifier's trace gains four non-numeric rows beside the eight axes: noun, quotation, clock, description-date. (4) The piece is read once against its own sources[] before the pair verdict, in both languages, asking only: does the body say about this register what the citation says about it? Forward surface named so it is not forgotten: the attribution verb — said · announced · confirmed · found · estimates each promise a different register, and nothing checks them.

Codified into the RUNBOOK — rule 2 of 2 · and acted on in the same commit

A hand-run assertion is a habit, not a gate — wire it or state why it is out.

A green check that costs a human's attention is paid for out of the runs that have least of it — and those are the runs that ship the defect. (1) An assertion that reads only web/dist is wired into CI in the same commit that proves it; there is no intermediate state where it "runs in the daily". (2) An assertion kept out of CI carries its reason in the workflow file, beside the ones that are in. (3) The weekly review prints the ratio — a count nobody prints is a count nobody notices drifting.

Action of record, today: qa_a11y_lang, qa_ar_language and qa_consumer_surface wired into the build job, each proved both ways per ruling #35 before landing — control exit 0 on the real 82-page build; bite exit 1 on an Arabic string injected under lang="en", on a Latin country name injected into Arabic chrome, and on an og:image reverted to SVG. 10 of 12 assertions now gate the deploy. The two that don't, don't for reasons now written in the file: qa_live_drift reads the origin (the verify job's byte-compare is strictly stronger) and qa_sources_alive reads third-party hosts (someone else's 404 is not our build's failure).

4509-08A number's noun is part of the figurePrice / saving / target / reached / planned / avoided are the figure. Two sentences with the same digits and different nouns carry different figures. The eighth trace axis: type. Two channels of figure checking cannot catch a type error — only a read of the source sentence can, so the trace quotes the clause, not the number.
4609-09A quotation is verbatim or it is paraphraseQuotation marks promise the register, not the meaning. An emendation — even a correct one — is marked, or the quotation's boundary moves. Why it isn't pedantry: an unmarked emendation is indistinguishable afterwards from the source having changed underneath us. Corollary, the clock: a plan is only a plan until its date arrives.
4709-10A description has a date tooEvery non-numeric attribute — what a programme taught, where it ran, what it was called, what prize it held, where a sentence was said — is dated as strictly as a figure and inherits its register's vintage. A description read at one stage does not describe another stage. A setting is a claim about a register, never inferred from the occasion.
4809-11A trend line can have a seamA long series is not automatically a series. Where the owner has spliced measurements of different populations, grades or scales, the join is a construction with a measured size, and that size rides with every figure crossing it. Prefer the owner's certified window. Corollary: one standard deviation on a scale fixed at 500/100 by construction is a scale unit, not an effect size.
