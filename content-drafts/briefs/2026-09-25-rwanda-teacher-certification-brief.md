## 1. The Editor's ruling on the three things the 09-24 re-verification asked for

The instruction *"TTC 3 → 5 years… Not yet in force; **write it in the future tense**"* is **struck
from the commission**, and the finding that struck it is recorded rather than quietly absorbed:
**a recon's drafting instruction went stale between banking and drafting, and a drafter obeying it

**What the drafter writes instead, and this is binding to the clause:** the five-year TTC programme

sentence carries that register and that date. **Do not write "began." Do not write "will begin."**

*Announced ≠ commenced* is this edition's own standing prohibition, and here it is wearing a
calendar.

### Ruling 2 — the three-way seam is a STANDING PROHIBITION on this piece. Accepted, and extended to four.

~25,000 *entered* under relaxed criteria (World Bank feature) · ~27,322 counted **untrained** in
2023/24 (MINEDUC Statistical Yearbook) · 24,000 trained **and certified** by Mar 2026 (World Bank

> **No sentence may place two of these three in a subtraction, a ratio or a share without naming
> both registers and both dates in the same sentence.** Ruling #14, and #19 puts the guardrail in
> the sentence that makes the claim, never in a note beside it.

**Extended to a fourth definition today** (§3 of the 09-25 hunt): **A2-qualified** is not

They are different sets and nothing read establishes their overlap. **The 27,322 may not be

### Ruling 3 — the A2→A1 instrument does NOT carry the ending. The recon's original ending stands.

The hunt is complete and negative: eight routes, four of them MINEDUC's own pages and one REB's
news index, and the instrument appears on none of them. It reaches us through **The New Times

**The call, and why it is not close.** An ending is load-bearing by definition, and #43 puts

ministry that publishes what it owes.

**What the drafter does with it instead.** One attributed sentence in the body, not the close:

**If a first-party register turns up before the gate**, the ending is re-opened, in writing, by the

## 2. Two guardrails added today, both from the 09-25 hunt

**GUARDRAIL A — the tier vocabulary is the newspaper's, not the ministry's.** MINEDUC's 10 August
register **never names A1**. Its own claim, verbatim, is that the longer programme *"will also help
bring Rwanda's qualification requirements for primary teachers closer to regional and international
standards, where a diploma or bachelor's degree is increasingly required to enter the profession."*
No tier, and the standard attributed to the region rather than claimed for Rwanda.

> **No sentence may attribute the A1 outcome of the five-year TTC to MINEDUC.** Every use of A2,
> A1 or A0 in connection with the 2026 reforms carries the paper and the date in the same clause.
> Where the ministry's own claim will do, use the ministry's own words.

This is #43 arriving as a vocabulary problem instead of a figure problem: the register we rank
lowest is the only one fluent in the terms the piece most wants to use. Say less, and cite better.

**GUARDRAIL B — the scope seam of the yearbook figures.** The 79,224 denominator (10,898
pre-primary + 68,326 primary, 2023/24) and the ~27,322 untrained are **the ministry's own count on
the ministry's own definition**, and that is exactly why they are worth carrying. They are *not* a
count of the special programme's intake, and no sentence may imply they are. The chronology is the
value: the programme opened **April 2024**, the 2023/24 school year ended **July 2024**, so this is
the debt as it stood essentially at the programme's opening, written down by the debtor.

**Carried unchanged from the recon:** ruling #32's scope seam (project 11,000 classrooms vs

## 3. The frame, and the five tests it must clear

**The argument in one sentence.** Rwanda built classrooms faster than it could staff them, hired
secondary-school graduates to fill them, **recorded them in its own statistical annual as
untrained**, and then ran a weekend-and-holiday certification programme that discharged most of
that debt — and the accomplishment is the bookkeeping, not the building.

**The sentence the piece is written toward**, because it is the one the yearbook gave us and the
funder's feature does not have: the ministry attributes the fall in trained primary teachers to
*"newly recruited primary teachers who may not have received training."* **The subject wrote the
debt down in its own voice.** Quote it; do not paraphrase it.

| Test | What clears it here |
|---|---|
| **1 · Sources read, not summarised** | Three registers re-read in served text 09-24; the Yearbook 2023/24 extracted and every carried figure confirmed twice; eight routes read 09-25. Every load-bearing figure on `mineduc.gov.rw` or the World Bank's own text. |
| **2 · A figure is cited as read** | The trained-teacher *trend* is deliberately not carried — the column alignment could not be confirmed. That omission is the test passing, and the draft does not quietly restore it. |
| **3 · The hedge survives its own paragraph** | The week of 09-21's named surface. *Announced*, *aimed to*, *may not have received*, *designed to* — each is load-bearing here and each must still be present in the **last** clause of its sentence, not only the first. |
| **4 · The absence is stated in print** | The pass rate is not published (route-2 precedent, ruling #11) and **the voice gap is stated**: every voice in the corpus reaches us through the funder's or the ministry's channel. Say so in the piece. |
| **5 · The positive posture does not deny context** | The debt was deliberate, the relaxation of hiring criteria was deliberate, and both are named early. A piece that reaches the certification without the shortfall is a press release. |

**Cap:** ≤2,300 words. **Posture:** positive, per the founder's 08-11 frame.
