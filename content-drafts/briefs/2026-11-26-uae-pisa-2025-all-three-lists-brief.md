# Editor's brief: Edition 06, piece 4. "The UAE on all three lists"

## The finding
Four systems are named on all three of the PISA 2025 release's improver lists, and **the United Arab
Emirates is the only one of the four outside Europe and the only one in this publication's own region.** It
rose in science over three years and over ten, and over ten it cut its share of low performers while growing
its share of top performers. That is three different tests passed, and piece 1 established that they are
three different tests.

## What makes it a piece rather than a line in piece 1
Piece 1 said the UAE is on all three. This piece has to say **what the UAE's own authorities said about it**
— which is a register, and the register has not been read. **Cell 2 does not open until a first-party UAE
statement on PISA 2025 is read in served text** (Ministry of Education, ADEK, or WAM for the date and the
quoted officials). The reachability table in
`content-drafts/briefs/2026-11-26-qatar-pisa-2025-two-of-three-brief.md` is the current state of that
attempt and is not repeated here.

## The Edition 10 boundary, stated now so it is not argued later
Edition 10 (28 December to 3 January) is the UAE with the focus on Abu Dhabi, and
`2026-11-22-abu-dhabi-irtiqaa-biennial-yield` is parked for it. **This piece is strictly the PISA 2025
reading at the national level.** It does not touch school inspection, the Irtiqaa framework, or any emirate's
own instrument. If the register that answers it turns out to be an Abu Dhabi one, the piece moves to
Edition 10 rather than widening here.

## Prohibitions
- Nothing about the UAE's reading or mathematics change. All three lists are about science (recon, #2).
- No ranking language. The UAE is not named in the OECD's top-ten sentence, and being on three improver
  lists is not being among the highest performers — that is the distinction piece 1 ends on.
- No public-source boundary breach: **public registers only, ever** (plan, Edition 10 note). No internal,
  unpublished or non-public material of any kind.
- Every figure carries its scale, its years and whose average it is.

## ADDENDUM, 2026-11-27 (daily run) — the register is read, and it carries a second finding the brief did not ask for

**Cell 2 is unblocked and the figures are now re-derived rather than banked.** Chapter 2 of *PISA 2025
Results (Volume I)* was parsed independently in this run, by a script that takes a table caption and a system
name and returns the row — no reading of any prior note. What it returns for the UAE:

| Table | UAE row |
|---|---|
| **I.2.6**, change 2022 → 2025 | science **+26**, reading **+15**, mathematics **+21** |
| **I.2.9**, fitted decennial trend since 2015 | science **18**, reading **−6**, mathematics **20** |
| **I.2.8**, change 2022 → 2025 in shares | low performers **−10.0** pp, top performers **+1.7** pp |
| **I.2.1**, science mean 2025 | **458** (OECD average 482, from I.2.3 and the front page's note 1) |

**The second finding, and it is the piece's spine rather than a decoration.** The brief commissioned this
row as *the UAE on all three lists* — a system that rose in all three subjects since 2022 and is on the
decade's science list and on the both-ends list. **Its fitted decennial trend in reading is −6.** So the UAE
is a system whose reading is up fifteen points against 2022 and whose ten-year reading line slopes the wrong
way, in the same chapter, in two tables one screen apart.

That is **not** the same piece as Qatar's. Qatar is a decade of movement and three flat years; the UAE is
three strong years inside a decade that is only strong in two subjects of three. The two pieces are the same
method on opposite arrangements, which is why both belong in this edition and why neither may borrow the
other's sentence.

**Carried from the Verifier's verdict on the Qatar piece, which found it and refused to print it there**,
because printing it on Qatar's page would have been the comparison across two questions that Qatar's own
brief prohibits. It is the finding *here*.
