## 1. The frame

**Mongolia has sat PISA twice.** Its own register says so — *"Монгол Улс 2022 онд анх удаа PISA үнэлгээнд
амжилттай хамрагдаж"* — and says why it sat it at all: General Education Law **article 35.6**, *education
quality is to be assessed every three years by an authorised international body.* Mongolia's participation
is a statutory interval, not a cycle-by-cycle decision.

Two cycles is enough to be measured and not enough to be trended, and Chapter 2 is built so that the
difference shows. **Mongolia has a row in nine of the chapter's fourteen numbered tables and no row in
Table I.2.9, the decennial trend** — and I.2.9's own note states both reasons: it shows only systems with
*"at least two previous assessments among PISA 2015, PISA 2018 and PISA 2022"*, and its trend is *"only
computed for countries with comparable data in at least three PISA assessments over the period
considered."* Mongolia has **one** previous assessment and **two** in total. It fails both.

And what the tables Mongolia *is* in print is this: **science up 12 score points, reading down 4,
mathematics down 6** — in the cycle whose major domain was science, the register's own cycle table saying
so, where the cycle before it was built around mathematics. **Mongolia's +12 is the largest science rise
among the thirteen systems whose reading and mathematics both fell.**

**That is the piece: a system two cycles old, measured in one subject's direction and not in a decade's,
and the only account that could tell a Mongolian reader what the country makes of it is announced, dated,
linked by its own author — and not served.**

### The one thing the piece must not do

It must not say or imply that Mongolia's science rose **because** science was the major domain. The
register prints the domain structure and prints the result; it does not join them, and neither does the
chapter. **The piece sets the two facts beside each other, names the question they raise, and says in
writing that nothing in either register answers it.** A causal sentence here is the fastest way to lose
this piece at the Verifier, and it is also simply not known.

## 3. Structure — about 1,400 to 1,600 words

1. **Open on the law, not the score.** Article 35.6 and the three-year interval: Mongolia sits PISA because
   a statute says the quality of its education is to be assessed every three years by an authorised
   international body. Then the two cycles, 2022 and 2025, with their samples.
2. **What the 2025 chapter prints.** The three changes with the average row beside each. Then the
   restraint: two of the three are falls and all three are better than the comparison group — *which is a
   sentence about the comparison group as much as about Mongolia.*
3. **The subject the cycle was built around.** The centre's own cycle table: 2022 major mathematics, 2025
   major science. Mongolia's mathematics fell between the cycle that was about it and the cycle that was
   not; its science rose across the same switch. **Name the question. State that neither register answers
   it. Stop.**
4. **The decade that cannot be asked for.** I.2.9, its note quoted, and the deduction. Nine tables of
   fourteen, the one absence, and why the absence is a fact about the instrument rather than a measurement
   of the country — the same shape as the Uzbekistan piece's two empty tables, reached from the other side:
   there the gate was the subject, here it is the clock.
5. **The gap that narrowed upward.** I.2.10 in full: 49 of 73, 34 against 15, and Mongolia among the 15
   with the average row among the 34. This is the edition's first piece on the release's headline equity
   sentence — *"largely because advantaged students performed less well rather than improvements among
   disadvantaged students"*, published here on 26 November — **counted against the table that sentence sits
   on.** Cross-link explicitly.
6. **Whose account.** What Mongolia measured that the OECD did not ask it to: a science-teacher
   questionnaire answered by 1,331 teachers and a parent questionnaire, both new this cycle, and the
   register's own reason — to read results against family, school and teacher conditions rather than at the
   level of the result. Then the national report: named *ПИСА-2025 Үндэсний тайлан*, dated September 2026 by
   its own author's timeline, linked from the page, **404 on three hosts in both encodings, and held by no
   archive.** The cause is visible and must be stated plainly as a cause, not as a suspicion: the paths are
   a WordPress tree and the register is now a Next.js application. **The register says the report was
   presented; this publication has no basis to doubt it and every basis to say it cannot be read.**
7. **Close on the asymmetry, not on the website.** Uruguay's own account was fetched and reproduced to the
   byte eleven days into this edition. Mongolia's is announced and not served. **One sentence on the name**,
   per §0's three rulings, and end.
