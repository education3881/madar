## Recommended frame — **"The teaching question"** (working title)
**One line:** who is allowed to teach, what the job has become, and what systems do when the teacher — not the test, not the building — is the binding constraint.
**Why this frame:**
- **Sequel logic** — Ed04 was the score on the child; Ed05 is the human who produces it. Elegant, and it gives the publication a spine a reader can follow.
- **Plays to our strengths** — "value of teachers" is already the corpus's #3 theme (22 pieces); "government-led programs" is #1 (30). Teacher-supply policy is government-led, globally comparable, and richly primary-sourced (national workforce plans, pay settlements, certification rules).
- **Bilingual native** — teacher-nationalization in the Gulf (Emiratisation/Saudization of teaching) is a first-class **Arabic** story, ADEK-adjacent (name the adjacency at commission, per the standing rule), not a translation afterthought.
- **Variety** — shortages, alt-certification, pay/status, workload/burnout, professional standards, and AI-as-substitute are distinct angles, so ten pieces won't read as one policy ten times.

**Alternate frames (kept warm, 2–3 seeds each):**
- **"The attention question"** — the global wave of school phone/screen bans and what reclaiming student attention costs (Australia, France, Netherlands, Brazil, China, UNESCO 2023). Very live, but risks being one policy repeated.
- **"The machine question"** — AI in the classroom after the first hype cycle: bans, reversals (we already have Korea AIDT), assessment integrity, teacher tools. `estonia-ai-leap` (carried from Ed04 bench) seeds this.
