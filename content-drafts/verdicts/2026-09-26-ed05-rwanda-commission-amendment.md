## Ruling 1 — the register of record for the denominator and the debt is the **2024/25** yearbook

The 09-24 re-verification reported the 2024/25 Education Statistical Yearbook as not existing and gave
the publication schedule as the cause. It has been on MINEDUC's own listing since **21 April 2026**.
The commission's Guardrail B, and every figure under it, is re-based on the newer register.

**What the drafter writes from, and this is binding:**

| | 2023/24 | 2024/25 |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-primary + primary teaching staff | 79,224 | **84,763** |
| Untrained, combined | 27,322 | **27,409** |
| Untrained primary (share) | 22,160 (32.4%) | **21,717 (30.2%)** |
| Untrained pre-primary (share) | 5,162 (47.4%) | **5,692 (44.2%)** |
| Trained primary | 46,166 | **50,162** |

**Both columns ship.** 2023/24 is the debt at the programme's opening — the chronology Guardrail B is
built on, and it keeps that job. 2024/25 is the latest state of the same count. They are one series in
one register, and a sentence using either names the school year in the same clause.

**Guardrail B's one-word change.** It reads *"this is the debt as it stood essentially at the
programme's opening."* Correct, and now the **first** of two observations rather than the only one. Add:
*and the same table's next column is the debt fifteen months later.*

## Ruling 2 — "discharged most of that debt" is STRUCK from the spine

The commission's argument sentence says the programme *"discharged most of that debt."* The ministry's
own annual for the school year ended July 2025 says the untrained count **declined only marginally** in
primary and **rose** in pre-primary, and that the combined stock went **up**. The clause cannot be
written, and no paraphrase of it may be either — not *largely retired*, not *substantially closed*, not
*paid down*.

**The spine, as amended.** *Incur, disclose, discharge at scale — and disclose again that the stock did
not move.* Four moments, not three, and the fourth is the one the piece is now written toward, because
it is the only one the ministry states about itself in a register the ministry publishes.

**The sentence the piece is written toward is replaced.** The commission sends the drafter to the
2023/24 attribution (*"newly recruited primary teachers who may not have received training"*). Keep it,
and add the 2024/25 register's own sharper version — *the persistence of a relatively high number of
untrained teachers*, attributed *particularly to newly recruited primary school teachers*. **Quote the
fragments the recon marks as unambiguous; do not compose a continuous sentence across the extraction's
column interleave.** If a continuous quotation is wanted, the page is re-opened and read; it is not
reconstructed.

## Ruling 3 — a FIFTH definition joins the seam, and the flow/stock prohibition is absolute

Ruling 2 of the commission runs four definitions deep (*entered* · *untrained* · *certified* ·
*A2-qualified*). The 2024/25 column adds **the stock at a later census date**, and the prohibition
extends to it word for word.

> **No sentence may place the 24,000 and any untrained count in a subtraction, a ratio, a share or a
> residual — in either direction, and however hedged.** 24,000 is a **flow** of certifications over a
> period ending March 2026; 27,409 and 27,322 are **stocks** at census dates. Ruling #68. Where the
> piece puts them near each other — and it should, because the juxtaposition is the finding — it says in
> the same passage that they are different kinds of quantity and that the workforce grew by 5,539 in the
> same year.

**And the tone of that passage is ruled now, not left to the draft.** This is not a debunking. The
registers do not disagree; they measure different things, and the ministry is the one that published
both. The nearest thing in our own corpus is the Brazil piece: *a state that prints the unkind number
beside the kind one.* Any sentence that reads as catching Rwanda out is returned.

## Ruling 4 — Guardrail A is REFINED, and one number is fenced

**The tier labels are MINEDUC's own.** The ministry's salary communiqué of **01 August 2022**, read in
served text today, uses A2, A1 and A0 and attaches a headcount to each. The 09-25 hunt's guardrail —
*"the tier vocabulary is the newspaper's"* — is withdrawn as written and replaced:

> **A2, A1 and A0 may be used as the ministry's own categories, cited to the 2022 communiqué.** No
> sentence may attribute **the A1 outcome of the five-year TTC**, or **the A2→A1 upgrade window**, to
> MINEDUC: MINEDUC's 10 August register names no tier at all. Both of those carry **The New Times, 24
> August 2026** in the same clause. Unchanged in force; narrower and correctly attributed.

**The salary figures the recon carried do not ship.** The communiqué gives a percentage and an increase
amount, never a before/after pair. The pairs in the 08-18 recon came from a secondary summary. What may
ship: the percentages, the increase amounts, and the tier headcounts, each with the communiqué and its
date.

**The 68,207 A2 holders is fenced.** It is the only quantity any register attaches to the A2 tier, and
it is August 2022, across primary and secondary, and the pay award's population. Carry it only with all
three qualifiers in the same sentence, and **never** in a subtraction or share with 27,409, 27,322,
24,000 or ~25,000. Nothing read says how many teachers the 2026 window covers, and the piece says so.
