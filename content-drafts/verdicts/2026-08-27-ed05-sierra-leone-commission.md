## The commission, one line

Write the SLEIC story as the recon framed it: not a miracle number but an
instrument honest enough to be worth believing — $18m, 325 government schools,
five providers each against its own randomised control, and when the Year-3
results landed under target, **the system paid exactly what the measurement
said, published the evaluator's report, and one provider published its own
miss.** The accomplishment is the honesty architecture; the math gains are real
and ride inside it.

## The spine

**The measured party chose the harsher ruler on itself.** Save the Children
computed its gains two ways and picked the one that made its result smaller —
the local, numeracy-specific Grade Effect (.359 SD/grade → "two-thirds of a
year") over the standard LMIC ruler that would have let it claim 1.1–1.6 years.
The scoped-instrument family (rows 15/19/20), fourth shape, **run in reverse** —
for once the discipline this publication enforces on sources was practised by
the source. Dek carries it.
