## What this piece is
The reform that abolished the single overall judgment rather than moving its bar. From 10 November 2025 England's Ofsted stopped issuing one summary word (*outstanding / good / requires improvement / inadequate*) and began issuing a report card — a grid of separately graded evaluation areas on a five-point colour scale (*urgent improvement / needs attention / expected standard / strong standard / exceptional*), under a "secure fit" rule where one missed standard drags the whole area down, with safeguarding met/not-met and inclusion raised to its own area. The Ed04 spine: us-naep is fifty-one rulers wearing one word; Australia is one ruler re-notched in public; the Netherlands points the ruler at the graders; **England takes the single number apart entirely, and then has to measure whether the replacement is better** — a task two rival instruments answer differently (a self-selected academics' survey now vs Ofsted's commissioned IFF evaluation in 2027). The natural fifth face of "what a score measures": what happens when a system stops issuing the score and measures the measurement instead. **Deliberate frame-pair with Oman** ("The verdict, made public" — Oman adopts the very single-verdict model England is dismantling); this piece lives entirely in the replacement's operating period (Nov 2025 → Jul 2026), one orienting sentence on the Ruth Perry finding, no re-narration of the retreat (recon §6 constraint honoured).
