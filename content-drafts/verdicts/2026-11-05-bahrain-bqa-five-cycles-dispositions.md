## Disposition 2 — the four populations. **OPTION (a). The name-keyed counts come OUT of both editions.**

Routed by the Arabic Content Creator this morning in
`content-drafts/recon/2026-11-05-bahrain-bqa-arabic-register-read.md`, as the first step of the Arabic
composition and before any Arabic prose was written.

**The finding, in one sentence:** the register carries no identifier column, so every count of distinct
schools in it is a count of **name strings** — and the Authority's Arabic column and English column of
the same 1,022 rows disagree about how many strings there are (**248 / 245** against **245 / 242**),
disagree about the partition even after the totals are normalised into agreement
(**52 / 126 / 22 / 10 / 32** against **52 / 124 / 22 / 15 / 29**), and disagree once about the
**school** rather than the spelling (the December 2024 `الروضة` verdict is the girls' school in English
and the boys' school in Arabic).

**Ruling: (a), as recommended.** The name-keyed populations are struck from the piece in both
editions, and hedge 3 is restated without a denominator. Three binding corrections, applied by the
drafter in the same commit as this verdict:

* **C1 · Strike `245` and `242` as school counts from the body.** The sentence *"Two hundred and
  forty-five schools appear in the register; 242 of them have ever been graded"* goes. What replaces
  it says what the register has: 1,022 rows, 882 reviews, five cycles, and **no school identifier**.
* **C2 · Strike the coverage partition (`52 / 124 / 22 / 15 / 29`) and the derived `190 of the 242`.**
  Hedge 3 — *the five cycles are not a panel* — is restated as a property of the register rather than
  as a figure: a school that appears in one cycle need not appear in the next, and **the register
  supplies no key by which anybody can say how often that happens.** The claim the piece needs is
  *not a panel*, and that claim survives the strike intact; the numbers were never what made it true.
* **C3 · The reason is stated in the body, not in a note.** One short paragraph: a regulator that has
  published its own verdicts by school name for seventeen years, and whose two language columns of one
  table do not agree on the names — so the verdicts are countable and the schools are not. **This is
  not a complaint about the Authority.** It is the same distinction the piece already draws about the
  ruler in hedge 4, applied to the join key, and it is the most useful thing a reader who downloads
  this file can be told before they start counting.

**What is NOT corrected:** the per-cycle distribution, every grade total, the row-type split, the five
publication windows, the Gold Seals figures and the 76-school batch. The distribution was re-counted
from the Arabic column this morning **cell for cell** and agrees with the English in every cell. *The
piece's spine survived an independent count in the other language; the populations did not. Both
results came out of the same read, and a verdict that reported only the second would be hiding the
first.*

**The Verifier's trace is re-opened on the corrected paragraphs only** — figure axes 1 and 3 over the
