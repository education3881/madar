**Raised by:** the Arabic Editor's Gate 4 verdict, 2026-11-09, §4.

## The finding, restated in the units that bind

**#114:** a figure printed only in an annotation is a **published** figure.
**#42:** a pair carries only what **both** languages share.

As the pair stood this morning, the Arabic annotation 1 published the register's full per-cycle ×
per-grade counts — twenty cells, plus the three-band monitoring scale — and the English annotation 1
published none of them, saying instead:

> *grade totals over the 882 reviews: `(3) Satisfactory` 350, `(4) Inadequate` 245, `(2) Good` 202, `(1)
> Outstanding` 85; **per-cycle counts as tabulated above**; publication dates 2009-06 → 2026-04*

**"As tabulated above" points at the draft's own table, which is not served.** A reader of the English
article page is handed a cross-reference to a document they do not have. That is a defect independent of
the asymmetry, and it is the kind this operation has twice ruled on from the other direction: a citation
that resolves to nothing is the 08-16 chrome 404 moved inside an annotation.

## The disposition

**The English annotation 1 carries the twenty per-cycle counts and the three monitoring counts
explicitly, in the register's own grade order, and the words "as tabulated above" are struck.** The
Arabic annotation stands unchanged.

**Direction matters and is stated, because the cheap fix was available and is wrong.** Symmetry could
have been reached by cutting twenty-three numbers out of the Arabic. That would have deleted the only
arithmetic evidence this operation has ever had that an Arabic edition was composed rather than
translated (Gate 4 §2), to make a complete edition match an incomplete one. **A pair is levelled UP to
the edition that read more, never down to the edition that read less.** Generalised from #101 clause 3
— *where two editions render one institution the forced rendering is the control* — to a quantity: where
two editions carry one register's counts, **the edition that counted is the control.**

## What is permitted by the licence, checked rather than assumed

Source 1's licence note is binding and narrow: the portal prints `Licence unknown` and carries no licence
statement, so *"counts are reported; the table is not reproduced, excerpted as a table, charted or
embedded."*

Twenty-three aggregate counts over 1,022 rows are **counts reported**. They are not the table: the table
is 1,022 rows of school name, date, type, cycle and grade, and no school name, no date and no row appears
in either annotation. The addition does not move the piece one step closer to reproducing the register,
and it closes the distance between what the two editions tell a reader. **Permitted.**

**And the same note binds the Designer**, which is why it is repeated here: *charted* is in the licence's
own list. The hero still for this piece may not draw the distribution. A still that renders five cycles
at proportional magnitudes is a chart of a register we are not licensed to chart. The brief for the still
says so in those words.
