reports the author's name and publication date with no biographical or affiliation line anywhere on the

**The fact itself is true, and it was located rather than assumed.** Le Nestour's own expert profile at
the Center for Global Development carries it in the author's publisher's voice — *"previously spent two
years as a technical advisor at PASEC"* — read in served text **2026-10-07**.

**Why this is not a note.** The body leans on it, in both languages, and leans *hard*:

> *He spent two years as a technical adviser to PASEC before writing it, which makes him about as
> informed a secondary as a secondary gets — and a secondary is what he stays.*
> *وقد أمضى عامين مستشارًا تقنيًّا لباسيك (PASEC) قبل أن يكتب ذلك، وهو ما يجعله من أعلمِ ما يكون مصدرٌ
> ثانوي — وثانويًّا يبقى.*

That sentence is **the piece's entire warrant for using a secondary's ruler at all** — the

> فمُسجَّلان في صفحةِ المؤلف الشخصية على موقع مركز التنمية العالمية، مقروءةً كنصٍّ مُتاح في 2026-10-07،
> وعلى هذا السجلِّ تستند جملةُ المتن لا على هذا.*

**This is a second instance of ruling #84, not a new number.** #84's corollary is that *a reciprocity
check has no arrow* — the body can be right while the annotation is wrong — and that is exactly the
shape here, with the two editions agreeing on the same error so no parity check could ever see it (#86:
parity is a floor, never a verdict). Recorded as a second instance deliberately, the way the 10-05 run
recorded its urllib finding as a second instance of #80 rather than inventing a number for it.

**The register is named in WORDS and not as an inline URL, and the reason is a rule rather than a
style preference.** `qa_sources_alive` and `qa_pair_frontmatter` both collect promises by reading
`url:` **fields**; a URL written inside a `note:` is a promise the operation's sweeps cannot see. Adding
one here would have created an unswept outbound promise in the act of repairing a mis-located one —
which is the orphan shape (08-17) pointed the other way. **Named here so the next run that wants to
cite a second register inside an annotation knows the cost: either it becomes a `sources[]` entry with
its own `url:`, or it is named in words.**
