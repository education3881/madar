## Verification finding (method, banked to guidebook as ruling #21)
**A recount is not a revision.** Two true figures can describe the same cohort at different vintages — the year-end snapshot the system of record keeps (71.5) and the later recount an actor announces (79.6). Before writing "revised," check whether the system of record adopted the number. If it did not, attribute the recount to its author and its date *in the sentence* — the reader with the register open must not catch the publication asserting a restatement the register never made. Sibling of #12/#12b (self-administered vs external; comparability is not independence) and of #19 (the fence lives in the sentence).
