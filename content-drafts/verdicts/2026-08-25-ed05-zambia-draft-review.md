### 3. Does it clear the positive posture without becoming promotional? **PASS.**

The accomplishment is stated as institutional and specific — a policy revocable
by memorandum converted into a right repealable only in Parliament, and the
accommodation definition that removes the residual barrier tuition abolition
left behind. The counterweight is carried without hedging, and the closing
formulation ("A law can remove a fee… What a law cannot do is teach a child to
read") is the edition's posture working correctly: **a verified win, with its
limits measured rather than asserted.**

The four clauses read out of the Bill text — the citizenship gate, the criminal
penalty, the accommodation definition, the s.119(2) carve-out — are the piece's
strongest asset, and none of them appears in any coverage of this Act. That is
the standard: **we read the instrument.**

### 4. Is the spine the question the commission asked? **PASS.**

The Grade-7 redefinition runs from the second paragraph to the last section, and
the dek carries it. The 2002 policy defined on *"grades 1 to 7"* and the 2026 Act
defining primary as 1–6 close the argument at both ends, as the commission
required.
