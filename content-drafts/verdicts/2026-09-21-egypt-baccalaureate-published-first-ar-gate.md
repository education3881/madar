## 7. One finding, and it changed the English

**The EN draft's "the 120-year-old examination" is struck, in both languages, and no number
replaces it.** The figure appears in no source this piece read and in no recon entry; it
reached the draft as the kind of ambient fact that feels too settled to check. Asked during
the Arabic composition — because Arabic makes you choose a construction for it and the
construction made it visible — the nearest served registers give the لائحة of 29 March 1887
and a first unified examination that year, which would be **139** years, not 120; the
Egyptian secondary stage itself is dated to 1825 under a different name. Neither is a
document, both are press registers, and **this operation does not repair one unsourced
figure with another** (#41, and the 09-11 precedent where 0.9 SD was struck rather than
corrected to 0.96).

So the sentence now reads *"the examination it now stands beside rather than on top of"* /
«الامتحانَ الذي صارت تقف الآن إلى جانبه لا فوقه» — which is the whole of its argumentative
work, and none of its arithmetic. **Routed to the Researcher, not blocking:** what is the
first-party document date for the Thanaweya Amma certificate? If one exists, the Editor may
restore an age at the gate.

This is the **fifth consecutive Edition-05 finding that is not a number** — and the second
found by a language crossing rather than by a verification pass (the first was the HSRC
attribution verb, 09-14).
