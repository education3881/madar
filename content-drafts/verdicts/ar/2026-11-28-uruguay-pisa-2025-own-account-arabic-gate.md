## 3. The four corrections

**None touches a figure's value and none touches a quotation.**

1. **The same three figures, from one sentence of one report, were rendered two ways in one piece.**
   Outside the Spanish quotation the body wrote `444,5` with the register's comma while writing `423.2`
   and `404.9` with periods. Inside the quotation the comma is correct and must stay — it is what the
   register prints. Outside it, the piece's own convention governs, and the English edition had already
   settled it by writing `444.5`. → `444.5`. **The decimal separator is the one piece of a foreign
   register that a composer carries across the quotation mark without noticing**, because it looks like
   fidelity.
2. **`«المنسّقة الوطنية»` in the annotation — an Arabic rendering of ours wearing the marks that mean *the
   register's bytes*.** This is the **third instance of one misuse in this pair**: the composer caught
   `«reporte»` and `«R eport e»` before hand-off and fixed them in the body, and the same hand missed the
   same thing in the annotation one screen later. → set as *المنسّقة الوطنية* with the Spanish token kept
   in guillemets where it belongs. *A composer who has just learned a rule applies it to the text in
   front of them and not to the apparatus around it; the annotation is the part of a piece that is written
   last and read least.*
3. **`وهذا درسُ هذه الطبعة آتيًا من جهةٍ جديدة` — a `حال` accusative hung on a nominal predicate.**
   → `يأتي من جهةٍ جديدة`.
4. **`كلتيهما مقروءةً` — singular agreement after a dual.** `كلتا` with a pronoun suffix is dual, so the
   `حال` is dual. → `كلتيهما مقروءتين`.
