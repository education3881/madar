## 3. The seven corrections

Five are grammar or usage and two are terminology. **None touches a figure and none touches a quotation.**

1. **The dek stacked two accusatives with no measure preposition.** `خفضت أوزبكستان حصّةَ المتدنّين في
   العلوم 46.1 نقطةً مئوية` puts `حصّةَ` and `نقطةً` side by side as objects of one verb, which forces the
   reader to decide which is the object and which the specification. → `بمقدار 46.1 نقطةٍ مئوية`. Dek
   156 → **163** of 200, still inside the cap.
2. **`أمضت … على عادةٍ واحدة`.** `أمضى` governs `في`, not `على`; `على` here reads as *spent a week on top of
   a habit*. → `أمضت … في اتّباع عادةٍ واحدة`.
3. **`وأوزبكستان في واحدٍ منهما لا` — a half-finished negation, and the worst sentence in the draft.** It
   renders *Uzbekistan is in neither* by putting the negative particle at the end, where Arabic cannot carry
   it; the clause reads as *and Uzbekistan in one of them, no*. → `وليست أوزبكستان في أيٍّ منهما`. **This is
   the sentence the whole piece turns on** — the two absences are its subject — and it was the one sentence
   whose syntax a reader would have had to re-read.
4. **`وصفُّ «OECD average-35» يقرأ 1.2 و−0.3` — a calque.** A table row *reads* a value in English; in
   Arabic `يقرأ` means *he reads*, so the row is made the reader. The rest of this edition's Arabic uses the
   nominal form (`وصفُّ … فيه`) and this clause had drifted from it. → `فيه`.
5. **`فُرجة` for *gap*.** `فُرجة` is an opening or a relief; the word for the thing this piece is about — a
   distance between two units of account — is `فجوة`, which is also the word the corpus uses for a
   socio-economic gap. → `فجوة`.
6. **A duplicated relative in the definition of coverage.** `حصّةُ مَن هم في الخامسةَ عشرةَ في بلدٍ ممّن
   يجوز اختيارُهم` runs `مَن` and `ممّن` over one antecedent. → `ويجوز اختيارُهم`. The definition is the
   qualifier the register attaches to exactly this figure, so it is the clause least able to afford a
   second reading.
7. **The composer's own hand-off miscounted its own list: *eleven-name company list* where the register,
   the English body and the Arabic body all carry TEN** — Chile, Israel, Iceland, Brunei Darussalam,
   Mauritius, Montenegro, Albania\*, Qatar, Greece, Thailand. The published text was right in both editions
   and only the hand-off was wrong, which is why this is a correction and not a defect: **but a hand-off is
   what the next cell reads**, and a wrong count in it is how a right count gets "fixed" at cell 6.
   Corrected to ten. *The figure in the artefact was verified three times and the figure in the note about
   the artefact was verified never — which is #114's subject one layer further out.*
