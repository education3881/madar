## §2 — #27's channel debt: the verdict's own flagged item, discharged register by register

### (a) Brown et al. — **DISCHARGED. Second channel secured, and our annotation is confirmed verbatim.**

The publisher answers **403 to every client available here** — `curl` and the rendered fetch, on both
`/doi/full/` and `/doi/abs/`. **Walled, not dead** (#70). The abstract was therefore re-read word for
word on an independent bibliographic channel, the **RePEc/IDEAS** record for *Journal of Development
Effectiveness* **15(3), 320–341, 2023**, which reproduces the publisher's abstract verbatim and names
all twelve authors.

It confirms our annotation **clause for clause**: *"significantly greater improvements in mathematics
competency, Arabic literacy competency, and psychological wellbeing of children aged 7–9 in Sudan,
compared to state-provided education for out-of-school children, 6 months after the start of the
programme implementation (n = 221)"*; *"33 focus group discussions and key informant interviews"*; *"we
found no difference between groups on child-reported hope, and we found evidence for a significant
negative intervention effect on child-reported self-esteem, though the psychometric properties of these
instruments were not adequate."*

**And the second channel produced a finding that is a warning rather than a correction.** A *third*
route — a Joint Data Center literature-review summary of the same paper — describes the self-esteem
result differently: *"Self-esteem improved in comparison villages but not CWTL villages."* That is a
**different claim**: a difference-in-differences where the comparison group gained and the intervention
group did not, against the paper's own *"significant negative intervention effect."* Taken as a
correction it would have **replaced a true sentence with a false one**.

> **Disposition: the publisher's own abstract, reproduced verbatim by an independent service, outranks a
> third party's paraphrase of it (#43). The paraphrase is not a second channel on the finding; it is a
> second channel on *somebody's reading* of the finding.** Recorded because the pull was real: the
> summary is clearer, more cautious-sounding, and wrong. **A second channel is only a second channel if
> it is reproducing the register rather than interpreting it.**

The same summary also supplies a design detail the abstract does not carry — that the comparison
villages *had* educational facilities and the CWTL villages had none. **Not imported.** Upgrading an
annotation from the weaker of two disagreeing channels is the defect this item just avoided, run
backwards.
