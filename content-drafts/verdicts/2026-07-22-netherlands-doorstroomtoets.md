## What this piece is
The rare reform that changed not the height of the bar but the *job* of the test. The old eindtoets arrived after the teacher's schooladvies had already settled a child's secondary track; the doorstroomtoets (Wet doorstroomtoetsen po, first sat Feb 2024) was rebuilt around one February window, a hard 15-March result, and a **legal duty to revise the advice upward** when the test outscores the teacher — machinery aimed at the long-documented under-advising of children of less-educated parents. Three cycles in the measured result is double-edged: the lever moves (revisions up from ~1 in 10 to ~1 in 4 of all advices) but who benefits still tracks region and class, and the most advantaged intakes convert qualification into revision most reliably. Ed04 spine: us-naep is fifty-one rulers wearing one word; Australia is one ruler re-notched in public; the Netherlands **points the ruler at the graders** and writes the override into law. The natural fourth face of "what a score measures."
