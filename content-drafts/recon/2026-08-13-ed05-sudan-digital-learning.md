# Ed05 recon 5 — Sudan: the classroom that was invented before it was needed
**Filed:** 2026-08-13 · **Researcher** (under the Editor) · Edition 05 (AFRICA) **mandated slot #2** — the NEW Sudan piece · positive/creative angle inside the war's reality (founder posture, 2026-08-11: the frame never denies context)

## The story, one line
A decade before Sudan's war closed half the country's schools, Sudan invented the classroom that doesn't need a school: **e-Learning Sudan** (2014) — a mathematics game on a solar-charged tablet, run by a trained community facilitator in villages that had no school to close — built by UNICEF, War Child Holland, **Ahfad University** and the Ministry of Education, trialled rigorously, and scaled into the global **Can't Wait to Learn** platform that in 2025 kept ~125,000 children learning through *other countries'* wars (Ukraine, Uganda, Lebanon). When Sudan's own war came, the no-school classroom came home: since the beginning of 2024, **699 safe learning spaces** have opened across the country, **60 of them digital-learning spaces reaching 12,660+ children (54% girls) in eight states** — displaced children in Kassala doing e-learning sessions in a girls' school converted to a *Makanna* while their own schools stand closed. The accomplishment is an idea: Sudan built, tested and exported the instrument for exactly this catastrophe — and a version of it is running, inside Sudan, through the war.

## ⚠ THE LINEAGE GATE (draft-blocking; today's method note)
The story's arc — "Sudan invented it, the world uses it, and it came home in the war" — turns on an unproven link: **no source read this run states that the wartime digital learning spaces (Let Us Learn, #3) run Can't Wait to Learn content.** The 2019/2020 registers tie CWTL to Kassala e-learning centres; the 2024 register describes tablet e-learning in Kassala Makannas under a *different initiative name*; War Child's 2025 CWTL country list *omits Sudan*. Same place + same tech + same look ≠ same instrument. **A lineage claim is a figure: it carries provenance axes (same programme / successor / rebrand / parallel) and must be traced verbatim like a number.** Until a served-text source connects them, the draft carries TWO true stories side by side — the invention that scaled abroad, and the wartime digital spaces at home — joined by place and idea, NOT by asserted program identity. If the link is confirmed, it's the spine sentence; if it isn't, the piece is still whole (the idea's homecoming needs no brand continuity — and the honest version may be the better story).
