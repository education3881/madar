## What the document says, where it is readable and quotable

### 1. The six levels, defined — and the bar is NOT where ADEK's news page puts it

> *"Inspectors will make judgements using a six-level scale. The six levels of quality on the scale are
> defined as follows:"*
>
> **Outstanding** — *"Quality of performance substantially exceeds the expectation of the UAE"*
> **Very Good** — *"Quality of performance exceeds the expectation of the UAE"*
> **Good** — *"Quality of performance meets the expectation of the UAE (This is the expected level for
> every school in the UAE)"*
> **Acceptable** — *"Quality of performance meets the minimum level of quality required in the UAE (This
> is the minimum level for every school in the UAE)"*
> **Weak** — *"Quality of performance is below the expectation…"*

**The phrase *every school in the UAE* occurs exactly twice in this document, once on each of those two
rows.** ADEK's news page says *"In line with national expectations, Good is the minimum standard for every
school in the UAE."* **Expected and minimum are two different rows of this table and the news page assigns
Good to both.** If the edition question resolves in favour of this document being the instrument ADEK
cites, that is the piece's existing finding — a page making a firmer claim than the instrument it names —
arriving a second time, **on the bar rather than on the cadence**, which is the half a parent acts on.
**Held until the edition is settled.**

### 2. The six performance standards and the 17 indicators — and they match ADEK's English face exactly

> *"School inspections are structured around six performance standards and conclude with an overall
> performance judgement. Performance standards: 1. Students' achievement 2. Students' personal and social
> development, and their innovation skills 3. Teaching and assessment 4. Curriculum 5. The protection,
> care, guidance and support of students 6. Leadership and management. The choice of these performance
> standards, underpinned by 17 performance indicators, is based on research into school effectiveness."*

ADEK's news page prints the same six, in the same order, as *"These standards are: Students' achievement …
Leadership and management"*, and its Arabic face prints them too. **The two registers agree here**, and the
Editor's verdict records that the hypothesis of an Arabic-only enumeration was checked and refuted.

### 4. Two more clauses worth the drafter's attention

> *"All schools are required to submit self-evaluation information prior to the inspection team's visit.
> This should be aligned to the performance standards and elements in this framework."*

> *"The curriculum has a very clear rationale aligned to the school, Emirate and national visions."*
> (and the same clause at each descriptor level)

four-owner reference entry — **consistent with, not evidence for.**
