## 4. Status as of 2026-07
- Distance cohort shrank ~103k year-on-year into 2025/2026 (MON data via RBC-Ukraine, 09.2025) — direction of travel is back toward in-person where shelters exist.
- MON Order № 1020 (07.2025) revoked the 2024 distance order and tightened conditions for distance classes; recognition procedure for learning acquired abroad adopted (CMU Resolution № 734, 23.06.2025).
- Kharkiv continues the underground build-out (first underground kindergarten under construction; city plans several underground schools per district). Attacks on education infrastructure continue — keep as context, not spine.

## 5. Proposed angle (the mechanism, no politics)
One ministry runs a single national curriculum in three delivery formats at once, and the format is assigned by infrastructure, not preference. In-person study requires a certified shelter; where shelter capacity covers only part of a school, the school runs "mixed" — Kharkiv rations underground rooms so each class rotates in every one-to-two weeks. Where no safe format exists — occupied territories, frontline zones, families abroad — the state platform (AOS) and formal distance classes carry enrolment, so a pupil in Kharkiv, Kherson or Kraków sits in the same system of record. The numbers make the machine visible: 2.3M in-person, 780k mixed, 396k distance, 302,889 abroad — one register, three pipes. The most concrete artefact is municipal: Kharkiv converted six metro stations and built seven purpose-built underground schools, effectively adding a new asset class to school infrastructure. The piece walks the decision chain: shelter audit → format assignment → rotation schedule → platform fallback.
