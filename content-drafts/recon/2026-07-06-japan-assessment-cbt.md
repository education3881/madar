# Recon — Japan: the national assessment's CBT transition
**Date:** 2026-07-06 · **Researcher → Editor** · **Status: CONDITIONAL (route-2)**

## 1. The instrument
- **全国学力・学習状況調査** (National Assessment of Academic Ability) — **MEXT** (総合教育政策局・学力調査室) with **NIER** (国立教育政策研究所, 教育課程研究センター). Annual April census of all grade-6 (小6) and grade-9 (中3) students; 国語 + 算数/数学 every year, 理科 and 英語 roughly every third year. 2025 scale: **28,149 schools, ~2.01M target students**. NIER canonical: https://www.nier.go.jp/25chousa/25chousa.html · MEXT portal: https://www.mext.go.jp/a_menu/shotou/gakuryoku-chousa/index.htm (both fetched)
- CBT runs on the **MEXCBT** platform over GIGA one-device-per-student hardware. Operative plan document: 「令和7年度以降の全国学力・学習状況調査（悉皆調査）のCBTでの実施について」(first issued Apr 2024; revised Sep 2024 and Jan 2026): https://www.mext.go.jp/a_menu/shotou/gakuryoku-chousa/1421443_00005.htm · overview PDF (Jan 2026 rev.): https://www.mext.go.jp/content/20260130-mxt_chousa02-000035736-01.pdf (fetched)

## 5. Proposed angle
Japan's national assessment is mid-metamorphosis, and the first subject to go digital (JHS science, April 2025) was also the first stripped of the metric the country has argued over since 2007 — the average-percent-correct. In its place: an IRT score of 505 on a deliberately unfamiliar scale, five bands, and 16 of each student's 26 items kept secret so the pool can be reused and equated. The medium changed what the ruler is allowed to say — no single national exam day (a four-day window instead), no per-item feedback on secret items, prefecture tables delayed two and a half months and wrapped in box plots to blunt the league-table reflex. The trade is explicit: year-on-year comparability and results returned before summer break, bought with opacity. Operationally it is a quiet success story — 0.19% of students hit trouble at two-million scale in 2025, and school-level failures fell from 19 to 7 in 2026's English round. The cliff is 2027, when reading and math — the subjects prefectures actually campaign on — leave paper. The mechanism story: change the medium and you change the measure, the calendar, and who gets to see what.
