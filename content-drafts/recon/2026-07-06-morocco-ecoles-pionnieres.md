# Recon — Morocco: the "écoles pionnières" (المدارس الرائدة) scale-up
**Date:** 2026-07-06 · **Researcher → Editor** · **Status: PROCEED**

## 1. The instrument
- **Name:** Modèle « Écoles pionnières » / مؤسسات الريادة (pioneer establishments), the operational core of the Feuille de route 2022-2026.
- **Issuing body:** Ministère de l'Éducation nationale, du Préscolaire et des Sports (MENPS), Rabat.
- **Date:** Launched school year 2023-24 (626-school pilot). Label "Établissement pionnier" created by **décret n°2.24.144 of 8 July 2024**.
- **Links:** Ministry programme page (FR): https://www.men.gov.ma/fr/%C3%A9tablissements-pionniers · AR: https://www.men.gov.ma/%D9%85%D8%A4%D8%B3%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A9
- Mechanism: TaRL-based remediation month (6 wks primary / 4-8 wks collège), explicit instruction, ~20 quality criteria audited for the label, teacher certification + prime, weekly inspector visits.

## 4. Status as of 2026-07
- 2025-26 ran with 4,626 primary + 786 collèges; third label round published 4 Dec 2025 (2,415 labelled). Ministry (under Mohamed Saad Berrada since Oct 2024) holds the ~2,000-schools/yr expansion toward 2027-28 full primary coverage.
- WB published a consolidated results feature 21 May 2026 (EN/FR/AR) — convenient, but keep ministry/CSEFRS/MEL as spine.
- Open debate in Moroccan press: when the model extends to lycées (SNRT News, AR: https://snrtnews.com/article/149238); parents' worry that levelled groups slow strong students, ministry rebuttal (Le Matin: https://lematin.ma/societe/ecoles-pionnieres-les-parents-sinquietent-le-ministere-rassure/310142).
- No reversals found; the risk story is dilution-at-scale, not rollback.

## 5. Proposed angle
The mechanism is the label, not the money. Morocco did not rename 4,626 schools "pioneer" — it built a certification gate: a school adopts the TaRL remediation month, explicit instruction, and a livret-based assessment regime; its teachers sit certification; then a central commission audits ~20 criteria and either grants the label or doesn't. The Nov 2025 round shows the gate has teeth — roughly one in six candidate schools of the 2024-25 promotion did not pass. The teacher prime is chained to the same gate, so the incentive points at practice change rather than enrolment in the programme. Around that gate sits an unusually crowded evaluation scaffold — MEL/UM6P impact numbers (+0.9 SD year 1), CSEFRS external fidelity review (79/100), ONDH qualitative work — which is what separates this from the region's announcement-first reforms. The scale question is the honest tension: what a gate can certify at 626 schools it must now certify at 4,626, with 75,000 teachers, on the way to 8,630. Close on Berrechid: a provincial director and a school director describing the audit-and-evaluate routine as ordinary work — deployment as administration, not ceremony.

## 6. Risks / guardrail notes
- Distinguish cohort counts (schools running the model) from label counts (schools certified) — conflating 4,626 with 2,415 would be the classic error here.
- +0.9 SD / "82% of peers" is a **preliminary** year-1 evaluation on the pilot cohort; label it as such; full RCT-grade publication not yet located.
- 56% (share of students) vs 54% (share of schools) circulate for 2025-26 — use WB's "~54% of schools / >2M students" pairing.
- Prime amount (10,000 MAD) not yet verified on a primary document — either verify in BO or attribute to press.
- CSEFRS 79/100 is a conformity/fidelity index, not a learning outcome — do not blend with MEL results.
- AR naming: ministry uses مؤسسات الريادة; press uses المدارس الرائدة — pick one, gloss the other.
