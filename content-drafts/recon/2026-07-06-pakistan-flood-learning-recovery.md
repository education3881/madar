## 2. Figures (each labeled MEASURED or ANNOUNCED; carrier = official readout via state wire, 23.03.2026, unless noted)
- **19,808 schools damaged** in Sindh by the 2022 floods — MEASURED (damage assessment)
- **5,465 schools taken up** for reconstruction/rehab (2,268 fully + 3,197 partially damaged) — MEASURED (programme scope)
- **14,343 schools still awaiting restoration** as of 23.03.2026 — MEASURED — the load-bearing delivery figure
- **2,114 schools completed** across all funding streams; ~1.4M students' facilities restored — MEASURED (completions) / ANNOUNCED (student figure, derived)
- Rs 167bn in reconstruction schemes; **Rs 63.95bn spent** — MEASURED (spend)
- Stream detail: ADP 2,405 underway / **617 completed**; M&R **938 completed**; SSEIP-FA 805 targeted (June 2027) — ANNOUNCED; Federal PSDP 481 targeted / **37 completed** (Dec 2026); EU–UNICEF **173 rehabilitated** — MEASURED where bolded
- CM directive that flood-school reconstruction be completed **"by end of 2025"** — ANNOUNCED, not delivered (see above) — Dawn: https://www.dawn.com/news/1862607
- Context, on true primaries: out-of-school children nationally **26.2M** (Pakistan Institute of Education, Pakistan Education Statistics 2022-23; via Dawn: https://www.dawn.com/news/amp/1975945); **Sindh ~7.82M OOSC**; flood-attributable OOSC change is not isolable in published PSLM/PIE tables — no pre/post flood OOSC pair exists on a primary source
- **TeleSchool** (MoFEPT): platform and app live — https://teleschool.etaleem.gov.pk/ — but no published 2025-26 usage/reach data — ANNOUNCED-tier only
- Historical UN operational anchor: 230,000 children in ~2,000 temporary learning centres, Sindh (UNICEF, Jan 2023): https://www.unicef.org/press-releases/education-hold-230000-children-pakistans-flood-affected-sindh

## 4. Status as of 2026-07
- Four school years after the floods: ~11% of damaged schools completed (2,114 of 19,808); 72% (14,343) not yet in any restoration stream. The end-2025 completion order was missed; official horizon has slid to mid-2027 (SSEIP-FA June 2027, PSDP Dec 2026).
- Sindh Budget 2026-27 coverage (voicepk.net, 06.2026) flags tightened development spending — reconstruction financing is under pressure, unresolved.
- 2025 Punjab/Ravi flooding created new school damage in another province — do not conflate with the 2022 caseload.

## 5. Proposed angle (if it were to run — it should not, yet)
The only honest mechanism story is the delivery-tracking machine itself: a province managing a 19,808-school caseload through five parallel funding streams, each with its own completion clock, reviewed against division- and district-level tables at CM level. But with 72% of the caseload untouched and the flagship deadline already missed, any accurate telling becomes a story about non-delivery — which in this theme reads as blame, not mechanism. The TeleSchool/EdTech stopgap layer, which would give the piece a continuity spine, has no published reach data for 2025-26. Park until the machine has something delivered to describe, or until the SSB (School Specific Budget) decentralised-repair mechanism surfaces auditable primary data.
