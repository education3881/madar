## 5. Proposed angle
When a school system loses its buildings, what is left is process — and Gaza's education actors have rebuilt schooling as process. A tent, a teacher on a cluster payroll, and a fixed timetable make a Temporary Learning Space: 5,180 teachers are running them for about 220,950 children, a third of the school-age population, with a digital self-learning platform reaching roughly 296,000 more. The most consequential mechanism is the exam: the Tawjihi is the single credential that moves a Palestinian student into university, and the ministry has re-engineered it — electronic sessions in 2025 for ~24,500 stranded candidates, and a June–July 2026 session run from tents and repaired halls that deliberately absorbs three missed cohorts at once. Exam officials describe the logistics plainly: chairs, stationery, minimum-standard halls. This is a piece about the machinery of certification and continuity — who counts the learners, who prints the exam, who accredits the result — in the hardest operating environment on earth.
