# Recon — Netherlands: the doorstroomtoets transition
**Date:** 2026-07-06 · **Researcher → Editor** · **Status: PROCEED**

## 1. The instrument
- **Doorstroomtoets** ("transition test," end of primary, groep 8) under the **Wet doorstroomtoetsen po**, in force from school year 2023-24; first administration February 2024. Replaces the eindtoets (incl. the Centrale Eindtoets/"Cito-toets").
- Bodies: Ministry of **OCW** (policy); **CvTE** (College voor Toetsen en Examens — from 2024 one single national norm across all approved tests, executed with Stichting Cito); **Inspectie van het Onderwijs** (monitoring); **DUO** (data). Six approved tests in 2024-25 (LIB/Cito, IEP, ROUTE 8, DIA, AMN, DOE).
- Official overview + data: https://www.ocwincijfers.nl/sectoren/primair-onderwijs/leerlingen/prestaties-eindtoets · mechanics: https://duo.nl/zakelijk/primair-onderwijs/leerlingenadministratie/schooladvies-en-doorstroomtoets/

## 2. Figures
- **The mechanical change (statutory, in force since 2023-24):** preliminary school advice (voorlopig schooladvies) issued 10–31 Jan → **single national test window, first half of February** → result (toetsadvies) by 15 March → **if the test indicates a higher track, the school MUST revise the advice upward** unless it argues this is not in the pupil's interest → final advice by 24 March → national secondary-school application week 25–31 March. Timeline for 2025-26 fixed by OCW 12 Jun 2025: https://www.nieuwsbrievenminocw.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/06/12/tijdpad-doorstroomtoets-en-overgang-po-vo-schooljaar-2025---2026-vastgesteld
- **Scale (MEASURED, DUO via OCW in cijfers, published Mar 2026):** 2024-25 cycle ≈164,000 pupils — LIB 80,151 pupils / 2,817 schools; IEP 64,756 / 2,461; ROUTE 8 8,659; DIA 7,877; AMN 1,764; DOE 1,074. (URL in §1.)
- **Upward revisions (bijstellingen) as share of all school advices (MEASURED):** ~10% before the reform → **27% in 2023-24 (first cycle) → 22% in 2024-25** — PO-Raad themarapportage on DUO data: https://sectorrapportage.poraad.nl/themarapportage-doorstroomtoets/ ; third cycle 2025-26 reported at **~22.5%** — https://www.nationaleonderwijsgids.nl/basisonderwijs/gelijkheid-blijft-uit-doorstroomtoets-2026-toont-hardnekkige-verschillen/ (confirm exact figure in the themarapportage before drafting).
- **The equity finding (MEASURED, Inspectie van het Onderwijs, De Staat van het Onderwijs 2026, 15 Apr 2026):** where the test shows a higher advice, revision follows in **8 of 10 cases in IJsselstein (Utrecht) vs 4 of 10 in Stadskanaal** — despite revision being legally required; inspectorate sees both over- and under-advising. Report PDF: https://www.onderwijsinspectie.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/04/15/rapport-de-staat-van-het-onderwijs-2026/84125-ivho-de-staat-van-het-onderwijs-2026-tg.pdf
- **PO-Raad rapportage doorstroomtoets 2026 (MEASURED, DUO data, 23 Jun 2026):** schools with the **lowest schoolweging (most advantaged intake) revise most often when pupils qualify**; provider differences narrowed but **equal mastery of reference levels can still yield different toetsadviezen** (IEP vs LIB); share of pupils qualifying for revision ranges from **<1 in 10 to >half per municipality** (highest where no havo/vwo upper school exists locally). https://www.poraad.nl/onderwijskwaliteit/overgang-po-vo/toetsing/rapportage-doorstroomtoets-2026-verschillen-tussen
- **Background gradient (MEASURED, CBS via OCW in cijfers):** children of higher-educated / higher-income parents receive vwo and havo/vwo toetsadviezen markedly more often. (URL in §1.)

## 4. Status as of 2026-07
- Third cycle complete: Feb 2026 test, results by 15 Mar, final advices 24 Mar, application week end-March — cycle ran on schedule.
- Inspectorate (15 Apr) and PO-Raad (23 Jun) both land on the same 2026 verdict: the lever works mechanically, but **who benefits from mandatory revision still tracks region and social background**.
- **Convergence back to one test (ANNOUNCED):** Parliament motion (D66/SP/GroenLinks-PvdA/DENK/JA21) urging a single doorstroomtoets provider by 2027 — https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/moties/detail?id=2025Z21893&did=2025D51746 ; cabinet position: one central test in 2029-2030 (state secretary: 2029 earliest) — https://www.poraad.nl/onderwijskwaliteit/overgang-po-vo/kabinet-wil-in-2030-terug-naar-een-doorstroomtoets
- OCW + Inspectie + PO-Raad pilot exploring a changed function for the test; sector advisory team on the po-vo transition reports Oct 2026.

## 5. Proposed angle
The Dutch reform is the rare case where a country changed not the height of the bar but the job of the test: from verdict to lever. The old eindtoets arrived after the teacher's advice had already settled a child's secondary track; the doorstroomtoets was rebuilt around one February window, a hard mid-March result date, and — the heart of it — a legal duty to revise the advice upward when the test outperforms the teacher's expectation. It was designed as machinery against under-advising, which Dutch data had long shown falls hardest on children of less-educated parents. Three cycles in, the measured result is genuinely double-edged: revisions jumped from ~10% of advices to roughly one in four-to-five, so the lever moves — but the inspectorate's 2026 finding is that the same entitled child gets the revision eight times out of ten in IJsselstein and four times out of ten in Stadskanaal, and the most advantaged schools revise most. The instrument changed; the gatekeeping culture around it moved less. Meanwhile the five-test market produced its own measurement problem — equal skill, different advice depending on which test your school bought — and the state is now converging back to a single central test by 2029-30, quietly closing the circle to something like the old Cito. The mechanism lesson for the region: a ruler can be pointed at the graders, not just the graded — and it will measure them, too.

## 6. Risks / guardrail notes
- **Heroverweging ≠ bijstelling:** qualifying for reconsideration (test advice higher) and actual revision are different rates — never mix them; the 8/10-vs-4/10 figures are revision-when-qualifying.
- The 27%/22%/22.5% series is share of ALL advices revised; state the denominator. Confirm the 2026 figure in the PO-Raad themarapportage (https://sectorrapportage.poraad.nl/p/Doorstroomtoets) at draft time.
- OCW's own caution: scores from 2023-24 onward **must not be casually compared with earlier eindtoets years**; provider scores sit on different scales (LIB 176 vs IEP 79 are not comparable numbers).
- PO-Raad is the sector council — near-primary (official analysis of DUO data) but not government; anchor load-bearing equity claims to the Inspectie/OCW URLs, use PO-Raad for the provider and schoolweging findings with attribution.
- A half-remembered "2024 norming error" did NOT verify: CvTE's official review (Terugblik normering doorstroomtoetsen 2024, https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2024D28951) reports norming proceeded without special incidents. Do not import that claim.
- Dutch terms need one fixed EN/AR gloss each (schooladvies, toetsadvies, bijstelling, schoolweging, havo/vwo tracks); keep a mini-glossary in the piece.
