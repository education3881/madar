# Recon — England: the report-card regime's first operating term
**Date:** 2026-07-06 · **Researcher → Editor** · **Status: PROCEED**

## 1. The instrument (official name, issuing body, date, link)
- **Renewed Education Inspection Framework + report cards**, Ofsted — confirmed 9 Sept 2025, in force from **10 Nov 2025**: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/education-inspection-framework/education-inspection-framework-for-use-from-november-2025
- Confirmation announcement (9 Sept 2025): https://www.gov.uk/government/news/ofsted-confirms-changes-to-education-inspection-and-unveils-new-look-report-cards
- Design: no overall grade; a minimum of six evaluation areas (incl. a new stand-alone **inclusion** area) each graded on a colour-coded 5-point scale — *urgent improvement / needs attention / expected standard / strong standard / exceptional* — under a "secure fit" rule (every standard in the toolkit must be met); safeguarding is met/not met. Published cards: https://reports.ofsted.gov.uk

## 5. Proposed angle (the mechanism story)
The single word is gone; this is the first measured term of what replaced it. From 10 November 2025 England's schools stopped receiving a verdict and started receiving a grid: at least six areas, five renamed grades, and a "secure fit" rule under which one missed standard drags the whole area down. The piece follows the mechanism through its first eight months of data: the opening 22 volunteer cards whose 150 grades clustered in the comfortable middle (and what volunteering does to that distribution); the scale-up to 1,360 schools by May; heads discovering that "expected standard" is a hard floor benchmarked to national averages — which, as two of the first-inspected leaders noted, half of schools cannot mathematically clear; and the courts declining, twice, to referee any of it. The kicker is the reform's own yardstick: this framework exists because an inspection was found to have contributed to a head teacher's death, and the first independent measurement of the replacement found 70% of inspected heads negative about its effect on their wellbeing — with Ofsted's commissioned evaluation not due until 2027. A regime built to measure schools more fairly is now itself being measured, term by term, and the early numbers cut both ways (more collaborative in tone; heavier in load). September 2026's context fix is the first in-flight correction — evidence the new ruler is already being recalibrated.
