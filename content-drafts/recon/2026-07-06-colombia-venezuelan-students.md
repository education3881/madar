# Recon — Colombia: Venezuelan students and the permit-to-enrol machinery
**Date:** 2026-07-06 · **Researcher → Editor** · **Status: CONDITIONAL (route-2)**

## 1. The instrument
- **Estatuto Temporal de Protección para Migrantes Venezolanos (ETPV)**, Decreto 216 de 2021 (Presidencia/Migración Colombia), operationalised through the **Permiso por Protección Temporal (PPT)** and the RUMV registry. Migración Colombia PPT page: https://portal.migracioncolombia.gov.co/en/attention-to-citizens/procedures-and-services/regularization-for-the-venezuelan-population/temporary-protection-leave-ppt
- Enrolment-before-papers rule: joint MEN–Migración Circular No. 016 de 2018 — schools must enrol Venezuelan children regardless of status; undocumented students get a temporary **NES code** in SIMAT, later swapped for the PPT (mechanism confirmed in LEE report, fn. 2 — see §2).
- Still open for children: minors linked to the school system (or ICBF programmes) can register in RUMV and obtain the PPT **until 30 May 2031** (Ámbito Jurídico: https://www.ambitojuridico.com/noticias/civilfamilia/menores-venezolanos-pueden-solicitar-el-ppt-si-estudian-o-estan-en-programas).
- Placement without certificates: schools run validation/placement exams under Colombia's evaluation framework (Decreto 1290 route), with secretaría guidance — the arriving-student mechanism.

## 5. Proposed angle
Colombia's answer to a decade of Venezuelan arrivals was to make the school register itself part of the regularisation machine. A child can be enrolled with no papers at all — SIMAT issues a temporary NES code — and the temporary-protection permit (PPT) then converts that provisional record into a regular one; by mid-2024, 62% of enrolled Venezuelan students held valid documents, up from 38% two years earlier, with more than 600,000 in school at peak. The mechanism story is this paper-to-permit pipeline: enrol first, regularise second, validate prior learning by placement exam when Venezuelan certificates can't be produced. Nine in ten of these students sit in public schools, mostly urban. The 2026 school year opened with the ministry running a plan de choque in the border departments — cupos, matrícula accompaniment, secretaría reinforcement — which shows the machinery being pointed at the pressure points rather than rebuilt. The open question the piece should carry honestly: whether the 2024 plateau in the register is absorption completing or mobility resuming.
