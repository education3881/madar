## 3. THE FINDING: the commission's spine is contradicted by the subject's own annual

The commission of 2026-09-25, §3, states the argument in one sentence:

> "…and then ran a weekend-and-holiday certification programme that **discharged most of that debt** —
> and the accomplishment is the bookkeeping, not the building."

Against the ministry's own 2024/25 annual, **"discharged most of that debt" cannot be written.** In the
school year that ended July 2025 — fifteen months after the programme opened in April 2024:

- untrained **primary** teachers fell by **443** (22,160 → 21,717);
- untrained **pre-primary** teachers **rose** by **530** (5,162 → 5,692);
- the combined untrained count **rose**, 27,322 → 27,409;
- and the ministry's own word for the primary movement is **"marginally."**

**And the registers are not in contradiction.** This is the whole of the finding and it must not be
written as an exposé. The two numbers answer different questions:

- **24,000 is a flow** — certifications issued over a period, reported by the World Bank and REB in
  March 2026.
- **27,409 is a stock** — the untrained population standing in the workforce at a census date, July
  2025.

A flow does not reduce a stock if arrivals continue at the same rate, and the ministry says in its own
voice that they did: the persistence is attributed to *"newly recruited primary school teachers."* The
workforce grew by **5,539** pre-primary and primary staff in one year. **Rwanda was certifying faster
than before and hiring untrained teachers at least as fast.** No subtraction between the two numbers is
legitimate, in either direction, and the piece may not perform one. **Filed as ruling #68.**

**The positive posture survives this intact, and is stronger for it.** The accomplishment was never
the discharge; the commission says so itself — *the accomplishment is the bookkeeping.* What the
bookkeeping now shows is a ministry publishing, in its own statistical annual, the number that
undercuts its own funder's headline, and naming the cause as its own hiring. That is a harder and
rarer thing than retiring a debt, and it is the same virtue this publication credited Brazil with:
a state that prints the unkind number next to the kind one.

## 4. Consequences for the commission, listed so the Editor can rule line by line

1. **Register of record for the denominator and the debt changes** from the 2023/24 yearbook to the
   **2024/25** yearbook. The 2023/24 figures stay in the piece — they are the debt as it stood at the
   programme's opening, which is the chronology the commission's Guardrail B is built on — but they are
   now the **earlier** of two columns in one series, not the latest register.
2. **The spine's third verb cannot be "retire."** *Incur, disclose, retire* is not what the annual says
   happened. What it says happened is *incur, disclose, discharge at scale, and disclose again that the
   stock did not move.*
3. **A fifth definition joins the seam** — the commission's ruling 2 already runs four deep (*entered* ·
   *untrained* · *certified* · *A2-qualified*). The 2024/25 column adds **the stock at a later census
   date**, and the prohibition extends to it unchanged.
4. **Guardrail B's chronology claim needs one word changed.** It reads *"this is the debt as it stood
   essentially at the programme's opening."* True of 2023/24, and it is now the *first* of two
   observations, not the only one.
5. **The voice gap is unchanged** and should still be stated in print.
6. **Blocker 3 (the QBE progress note's date) is STILL OPEN, 39 days on** — re-read today, the page
   serves no publication date, byline date or timestamp of any kind. Ruling #32's scope seam re-read
   and confirmed as served.

## 5. Two registers read today that the recon had flagged as unread, and one of them refines a guardrail

### (a) The 2022 salary communiqué — READ in served text, and the recon's figures are not in it

The 08-18 recon lists this as *"Located, not yet read in served text — read before any figure ships"*
and records the award as *"A2 primary +88% (net Frw 57,639 → 108,488), A1 +40% (136,895 → 191,811), A0
176,189 → 246,384."* Read today, `mineduc.gov.rw`, dated in its own closing line **"Done at Kigali, on
01 August 2022"**, the communiqué carries:

| Tier | Teachers | The award as served |
|---|---|---|
| A2 certificate holders | **68,207** | *"increase by 88% of net starting salary or 50,849 FRW"* |
| A1 diploma holders | **12,214** | *"increase by 40% of net starting salary or 54,916 FRW"* |
| A0 degree holders | **17,547** | *"increase by 40% of net starting salary or 70,195 FRW"* |

**The before/after pairs in the recon are not in the ministry's register.** It gives a percentage and
an increase amount; it does not print the starting or the resulting salary. The recon's pairs came from
a secondary summary, and **they do not ship.** (57,639 × 0.88 = 50,722, not 50,849, which is the tell.)

### (b) GUARDRAIL A is REFINED — the tier vocabulary is the ministry's own; what is the newspaper's is narrower

The 09-25 hunt's guardrail says *"the tier vocabulary is the newspaper's, not the ministry's."* That is
too broad, and the correction makes the guardrail sharper rather than looser. **MINEDUC's own 2022
communiqué is fluent in A2, A1 and A0, and attaches a headcount to each.** The tiers are the ministry's
established pay-scale categories, not one paper's coinage.

What reaches us **only** through The New Times remains exactly what the hunt found, and it is the part
that matters: that the five-year TTC **yields an A1**, and that serving A2 holders have five years to
reach that tier. MINEDUC's 10 August register names no tier at all. **So the guardrail becomes:** the
tier *labels* may be used as the ministry's own, sourced to the 2022 communiqué; **no sentence may
attribute the A1 outcome of the five-year TTC, or the upgrade window, to MINEDUC** — those carry The
New Times and 24 August 2026 in the same clause. Unchanged in force, narrower in scope, and now
correctly attributed.

**And a number, with a fence around it.** The 68,207 A2 holders is the only quantity any register has
ever attached to the A2 tier — but it is **August 2022**, it is **across primary and secondary**, and
it is the **pay award's** population. Nothing read says how many teachers the 2026 upgrade window
covers. If the piece carries it, it carries all three of those qualifiers in the same sentence, and it
is never placed in a subtraction or a share with the 27,409, the 27,322, the 24,000 or the ~25,000.
