# Recon — Singapore: PSLE Achievement Levels + Full Subject-Based Banding
**Date:** 2026-07-06 · **Researcher → Editor** · **Status: PROCEED**

## 1. The instrument
- Primary School Leaving Examination (PSLE), scored since **2021** in **8 Achievement Levels** (AL1 best–AL8) per subject; **PSLE Score = sum of 4 subject ALs, range 4–32, lower is better** — replaced the T-score aggregate. Conducted by the **Singapore Examinations and Assessment Board (SEAB)**; scoring/posting policy by **MOE**.
- Change announced **13 Jul 2016**, MOE press release "Changes to PSLE Scoring and Secondary One Posting": https://www.moe.gov.sg/news/press-releases/20160713-changes-to-psle-scoring-and-secondary-one-posting (fetched)
- **Full Subject-Based Banding (FSBB):** Express/N(A)/N(T) streams removed from the **2024 Sec 1 cohort**; subjects taken at G1/G2/G3; S1 posting via **Posting Groups 1/2/3**. Canonical microsite: https://www.moe.gov.sg/psle-fsbb/ (fetched)
- First FSBB cohort sits the new **Singapore-Cambridge Secondary Education Certificate (SEC) in 2027**, "jointly examined and awarded by SEAB, MOE, and Cambridge" (SEAB SEC page, updated 14 May 2026; fetched).

## 4. Status as of 2026-07
- First FSBB cohort (2024 S1) is now **Sec 3**; sits the first **SEC in 2027** (EL/MTL papers from September, others from October); results **Jan 2028 in one common release** — previously N-Levels (Dec) and O-Levels (Jan) were separate events. MOE COS 2026 press release (3 Mar 2026) + SEAB SEC page (both fetched).
- ANNOUNCED: new **Post-Secondary Admissions Exercise (2028)** — one Singpass portal replacing JAE/PFP-AE/ITE exercises; tie-breakers extend the S1 logic upward: citizenship → choice order → aggregate → computerised ballot.
- 2026 S1 cohort entered Jan 2026 (141 schools; posting released 19 Dec 2025); ~65% more-demanding-level eligibility steady two years running.
- Adjacent: GEP discontinued in current form, refreshed approach from 2027 (COS 2026) — same "less fine sorting" narrative arc, usable as context.

## 5. Proposed angle
In 2021 Singapore deliberately blunted its most famous ruler — from 200+ T-score gradations that ranked every twelve-year-old against the cohort to 29 possible scores that record only absolute mastery. The target was the measurement itself: MOE's first stated aim was to "reduce fine differentiation of students." But precision did not disappear; it migrated into the posting system — ties once invisible inside decimal points now surface as citizenship checks, choice-order weighting and computerised ballots, and cut-offs cluster so tightly that primary-school affiliation is worth about seven points in PG3. FSBB then removed the stream label while keeping per-subject sorting — in the minister's own words, "not really the end of streaming, but refinement of streaming by subject." The first unstreamed cohort is now in Sec 3 and will print the new ruler's first output, the single SEC certificate, in 2027. The mechanism story: when a system coarsens its measure, the sorting load does not vanish — it moves to the next available margin, and Singapore's own documents let us watch exactly where it went.

## 6. Risks / guardrail notes
- Do not overstate balloting: it is a third-order tie-breaker; the "1 in 10" is 2022-vintage and its base (tie-broken students, not cohort) must be stated if used.
- The Dec posting press releases contain no outcome percentages — cite the Jan 2025 parliamentary reply and the FAQ, not the December releases.
- Per-school COPs: SECONDARY ONLY as of now; verify on SchoolFinder before naming any school's cut-off.
- Do not claim the AL change reduced stress — Chan himself concedes the limit ("internal relativities" quote); keep the piece mechanistic, not celebratory.
