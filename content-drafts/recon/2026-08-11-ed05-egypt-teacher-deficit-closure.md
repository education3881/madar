# Ed05 recon 3 — Egypt: the deficit that closed without the hires
**Filed:** 2026-08-11 · **Researcher** (under the Editor) · Edition 05 candidate #5 (Egypt mass teacher-hiring drive, from the `_EDITION_05_PLAN.md` slate) · **Third recon of the Ed05 bank** · the slate's **second native-Arabic** story · no adjacency nod required

## The story, one line
In December 2025 Egypt's education minister declared a teacher deficit of 460,000 **eliminated** in roughly one school year — not by appointing 460,000 teachers (the presidential hiring drive appoints ~30,000 a year, and fewer passed than that), but by three different instruments at once: a permanent-appointment competition, **160,000 per-session teachers paid by the lesson**, and **arithmetic** — a longer school year, more teaching weeks, and a lower weekly quota per teacher. The piece is the anatomy of a deficit: a deficit is a *ratio* (hours required ÷ hours a teacher supplies), and Egypt moved every term of the ratio. Closing a deficit of *what*?

Whether this drafts as the counter-piece or as a redefined-denominator story is an **evidence call at draft, not a frame call now**. The recon holds both.

## Load-bearing figures — axes flagged (rulings #12–#28)
**Served-text confirmed (sources 1–2, quoted verbatim this run):**
- **"Every core subject classroom now has a teacher"** — the strongest sentence in the corpus, in the minister's own byline. Scope axis (#22): *core subject* is the load-bearing qualifier — which subjects are core, and what of the rest? The dataset behind it is **the ministry's own, submitted through CAPMAS** — administration axis (#12): self-reported national data, not an external audit; the piece must carry that in-sentence.
- **The presidential drive (via EIPR):** 150,000 over five years under the President's auspices; phase 1 = **90,000 sat the CAOA test, 28,000 passed** (against a 30,000 target); a **second screening at the Military Academy** — medical, physical (incl. weighing, 100m in 20s), assessment — that EIPR documents as excluding thousands, incl. for **overweight, pregnancy and childbirth**, and calls a violation of the constitution; court appeals proceeding (commissioners' reports in the appellants' favour, Apr 2024). Funnel axis (#14): **target ≠ sat ≠ passed ≠ screened ≠ appointed** — five different numbers, never chain them; the *appointed* count is the one not yet located.
- **Hegazy (predecessor minister, via EIPR): 20,000/year needed** — note this is *replacement-rate* scope (retirements), a different instrument from the 460k stock figure. #22 again: two deficit concepts, don't blend.
- **1997 Ganzouri hiring freeze** — the origin of the accumulated stock; gives the piece its 25-year spine.

**Search-stage (leads — NOT citable until served-text traced at draft):**
- **460,000 eliminated (Dec 2025)** vs **469,000 plan figure** — the headline number drifts 9,000 between registers; vintage axis (#21): which is the system-of-record figure, dated when? The deficit's *formula* (required hours ÷ weekly quota, at what class density) appears in no located primary — **finding whether one exists is draft-blocking homework**. A deficit with no published formula may itself be the sharpest sentence.
- **160,000 معلمو الحصة** (Dec 2025 claim) vs **50,000 per-session contracts** (plan register) — different vintages *and* different scopes (working headcount vs contracted intake); position axis (#24): never present as one series.
- **The denominator moves** — longer school year, more teaching weeks, lower weekly نصاب per teacher (Dec 2025 remarks as reported). These close a *ratio* deficit without adding a person. No single primary yet located states the arithmetic; chase MoE circulars on the quota.
- **EGP 1,000 teaching incentive** — conditioned on teaching in-class (#25 condition: an incentive *for those who teach* is also a signal of who wasn't).
- **Attendance "above 80%"** — GEM-blog-carried, ministry-dataset-derived (#12). Read the blog's own hedges before the figure rides.
- Phase 2 of the drive: **20,866 passed** (Egyptian Streets) — same funnel discipline; and the 2025 competition's waiting lists still being drawn down in Jul 2026 says the appointment pipeline runs *years* behind the claim of closure — that gap is usable, and checkable.

**Misattribution traps (flagged now):** the 90k/28k/20,866 funnel figures ride on EIPR/coverage attribution, not yet on a CAOA/ministry primary; the Dec 2025 "eliminated" claim lives in coverage of remarks — find whether SIS/cabinet carried it in the state register before it rides as more than reported remarks (#19/#26 family).
