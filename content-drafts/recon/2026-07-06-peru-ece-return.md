# Recon — Peru: the census evaluation's return

**Date:** 2026-07-06 · **Researcher → Editor** · **Status: CONDITIONAL (route-2)**

## 1. The instrument
- **Name discipline first**: the Evaluación Censal de Estudiantes (ECE) has not returned under that name. The pandemic broke the series (suspensions logged at http://umc.minedu.gob.pe/suspensiondeevaluaciones/); the successor is the **Evaluación Nacional de Logros de Aprendizaje (ENLA)**, run by **Minedu's UMC** (Oficina de Medición de la Calidad de los Aprendizajes) — http://umc.minedu.gob.pe/enla/
- **The census component returned once**: ENLA 2024 (applied 2024-11-19/20) included a **census of 4.º primaria — the first census application in six years** — plus a 6.º primaria sample. ENLA 2025 (applied Nov 2025) reverted to **sample-based** across 4.º primaria, 2.º secundaria and 5.º secundaria (5S newly added), with a socioemotional-skills module.
- School-level results feed back through **SICRECE** (https://sicrece.minedu.gob.pe/) with portals for directors, teachers and families. Results hub: http://umc.minedu.gob.pe/resultadosenla2025/

## 2. Figures
- **ENLA 2024 census scale — MEASURED**: 532,000 students of 4.º primaria in **18,112 public and private schools** (census); 105,534 students sampled in 6.º primaria. UMC, 2025-04-08: http://umc.minedu.gob.pe/enla-2024-532-000-escolares-de-4-o-grado-de-primaria-muestran-recuperacion-en-lectura-y-matematica/
- **ENLA 2024 results — MEASURED** (same URL): 4P Reading average exceeded 2023, 2022 **and 2019** (pre-pandemic); Maths exceeded 2023 and 2022. Share of students below elementary learning for the grade down to **3.4% — lowest since 2016**. Rural schools: **+24 points Reading, +22 Maths**; lowest band down **23.6% → 9.6%** (Reading) and **36.8% → 28.7%** (Maths). Loreto and Ucayali most improved; Tacna, Moquegua, Arequipa lead nationally. 6P unchanged vs 2022.
- **ENLA 2025 results — MEASURED**: nationally similar to 2023/2024; public and rural 4P schools improved vs 2024; **2.º secundaria Maths posts its first positive change since the school closures** (vs 2023); 5.º secundaria measured for the first time; socioemotional module: **70% of 2S students** self-report empathy/teamwork tools. Áncash and Ucayali advance in primaria, Puno in secundaria; Moquegua, Tacna, Arequipa still lead. UMC, 2026-05-12: http://umc.minedu.gob.pe/enla-2025-se-mantienen-avances-en-lectura-y-matematica-con-mejoras-en-las-zonas-rurales/
- Exact satisfactorio percentages by grade/subject: in the ENLA 2025 executive-summary and infographic PDFs (primaria: http://umc.minedu.gob.pe/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Resultados-ENLA-2025-Primaria.pdf · secundaria: http://umc.minedu.gob.pe/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Resultados-ENLA-2025-Secundaria.pdf) — pull at draft time.

## 4. Status as of 2026-07
- ENLA 2025 results published in tranches: results page live Feb 2026, infographics April 2026, national press note **2026-05-12**. Regional pedagogic reports per DRE/GRE are downloadable — the sample now drives regional, not school-level, action.
- The census was not repeated in 2025; no public commitment found on when (or whether) a census returns. That open question is part of the story.
- Measurement continuity has held across heavy political churn (three education ministers across the 2024–26 window; Quero → Cuadros captured in these two releases alone).

## 5. Proposed angle
Peru's story is the difference between a mirror for the system and a mirror for each school. The census instrument came back exactly once: in November 2024, after six years, every 4.º-grade classroom in 18,112 schools sat the same test, and the results carried school-level weight — each school could see itself in SICRECE, and the recovery (rural schools +24 in Reading, the lowest band cut from 23.6% to 9.6%) had an address, not just a national average. In 2025 the ruler stepped back to samples — statistically sufficient for the country, silent for the individual school. That trade is the mechanism: a census costs vastly more and returns a report card per school; a sample costs less and returns a report card per region. Peru is currently choosing per-region, while adding what the old ECE never had — a 5.º-secundaria exit measurement and a socioemotional module. The piece asks the measurement question directly: who is the ruler for — the ministry, the region, or the school gate?
